Proceed with removal 1032D12 timing system protective cover(s) - r + r .

1. Loosen the bolts fixing the timing belt moving tensioner.

2. Prise off and remove the timing belt.

Instructions for correct checking of toothed timing belt condition:

Check that the timing belt does not show signs of loose threads or cracks.

Check for 'black dust' along the entire length of the belt, on the toothed wheels, the shields, etc.

Visually inspect the belt teeth for check for any wear of the protective outer fabric.

If one or more of the above conditions indicating excess wear is found, replace the timing drive belt. .

The entire length of the belt should be inspected.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
