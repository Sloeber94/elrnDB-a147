A high frequency noise is heard from the engine, whose intensity varies with the number of revs

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the auxiliary drive belt and check that the tension is correct.

See if the whistling stays constant as more electrical loads are conneced.

Continue to Step 1

Belt worn.

Replace the belt Op. 1092G10 single engine belt r r

Tension incorrect

Adjust belt tension

The whistling noise increases.

Continue to Step 1

CHECKING ALTERNATOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the alternator is working properly, in particular

  • voltage regulator
  • the connections between the alternator and the battery

Continue to Step 2

Defective voltage regulator

Replace the alternator Op. 5530A10 alternator - r+r

Connections not correct

Restore connections

CHECKING AUXILIARY DRIVE BELT

Operation

Do not let the engine run for long with the belt disconnected
