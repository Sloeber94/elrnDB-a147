COMPONENT DESCRIPTIONS

Fuel filter

The fuel filter is fitted in the engine bay on the bulkhead.

It is the cartridge type with a filter element (1) made up of a pack of paper discs with a filtering surface area of 5300 cm2 and a filtration degree of 4 - 5 μm.

A sensor that detects the presence of water in the fuel filter is fitted at the base of the cartridge (2).
