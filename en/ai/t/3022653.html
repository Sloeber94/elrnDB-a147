Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the fuel gauge indicator is not working properly (it indicates a different level from what is actually in the tank)

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer

Continue to Step 2

Sensor with broken circuit, sensor short circuited, value inconsistent/implausible in relation to actual fuel level.

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket.

Check, by active diagnosis, that the pointer is working

Continue to Step 3

The pointer does not move

Panel defective, replace the instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

3

FUEL LEVEL SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the fuel level sensor.

Continue to Step 4

Sensor not working

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

4

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity/insulation of the cable between the fuel level sensor and the Body Computer : See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 5

Wires broken/not insulated

Restore cable continuity/insulation: See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Body Computer faulty

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r
