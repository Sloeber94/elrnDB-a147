Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When operating the alarm with the remote control:

  • the LED does not flash,
  • the doors do not open/close
  • the lights do not flash
  • the siren does not emit auditory signals

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

The activation of the auditory signals and flashing of the direction indicators depends on the various country modes.

For more details Characteristic of working principle 5580E anti-theft device

First check:

  • charge of remote control battery incorporated in the key
  • key correct, i.e. remote control enabled

Continue to Step 1

Remote control battery run down

Replace the remote control battery

Key not correct

Connect the test equipment to the tester connection and carry out correct memorization of the remote control

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and check in order:

  • whether there are faults in the alarm system

Continue to Step 2
