Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

When the boot is opened the luggage compartment light is turned on which goes out when the boot is closed.

The light can be left on by operating the switch on the actual light.

A light inside the glove compartment is turned on when the compartment is opened, and goes off when the compartment is closed.

The lights under each of the two sun visors are operated using the switch on them.

The Body Computer continuously acquires the 'battery voltage' signal and compares it with a limit: if the battery voltage level goes below this limit, it intervenes through the CAN sending a signal to disable a series of loads: these include the glove compartment light, the luggage compartment light and the sun visor lights; the function is activated with the ignition key in the OFF position.
