Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

ATTENTION

The engine compartment contains numerous moving parts, components at high temperatures and electrical wiring with voltage.

Follow these precautions scrupulously when carrying out operations in the engine compartment:

  • Turn off the engine and wait for it to cool down.
  • Do not smoke.
  • Do not use naked flames.
  • Make sure that there is a fire extinguisher in the vicinity.

WARNINGS

During each refitting, replace the gaskets, oil seals, spring washers, safety plates, self-locking nuts and all components which have deteriorated.
