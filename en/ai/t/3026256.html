Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The centralised EOBD tester connector is built into Body Computer M1 - connector G.

it is supplied directly at pin 16 from a line from fuse F39 of underfacia control unit B2; pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

Pin 1 is connected by line K to the ABS control unit M50 E7020 ABS

Pin 3 is connected by line K to the Air Bag control unit M60 E7030 AIRBAG

Pin 7 is connected by line K to the engine management control unit M10 See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT orE5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Pin 7 is also connected to the SELESPEED control unit M54 if fittedE7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

If fitted, the gas discharge headlamp control units F10 and F11 are connected to pin 12 E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

Pins 6 and 14 are connected to the B-CAN network. Underfacia control unit B2 , instrument panel E50 , door control unit H44 , rear electric windows control unit M82, radio (or built in radio-telephone-navigator) P20 and climate control ECU M70 are monitored and 'diagnosed' via this network.

Pins 2 and 10 are supplied for testing via the C-CAN network, which is not used in this version.
