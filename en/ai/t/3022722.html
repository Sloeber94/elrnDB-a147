CHECK ON CONDITION OF ENGINE SUPPORTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the correct fixing and condition of:

  • engine supports
  • flexible mountings
  • reaction rod

Continue to Step 2

Engine supports fixing not correct

Restore the correct fastening

Gearbox side engine support defective

Replace the gearbox side engine support Op. 1008A42 gear box left rigid mount - r r

Timing side engine support defective

Replace the timing side engine support Op. 1008A46 cam shaft left rigid mount - r r

Front support flexible mounting faulty

Replace the front support flexible mounting Op. 1008B10 power unit front mount (timing side) flexible block - replace

Rear support flexible mounting faulty

Replace the rear support flexible mounting Op. 1008B20 power unit intermediate mount flexible block - replace

Gearbox side support flexible mounting faulty

Replace gearbox side support flexible mounting Op. 1008B30 gearbox side mount flexible block - replace

Reaction rod fixing not correct

Restore the correct fastening

CHECKING ENGINE EXHAUST

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the engine exhaust system and, in particular:

  • pipe
  • exhaust silencer
  • catalytic converter front/rear gasket

Continue to Step 4

Pipe damaged

Replace the pipe Op. 1076B12 exhaust pipe assembly r+r emission control versions

Intermediate silencer damaged

Replace the intermediate silencer Op. 1076B26 intermediate exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions

Rear silencer damaged

Replace the rear silencer Op. 1076B22 rear exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions

Front gasket worn or damaged

Replace the front gasket Op. 1076B34 front catalytic converter attachment seal - replace

Rear gasket worn or damaged

Replace the rear gasket
