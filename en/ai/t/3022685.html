N11 RADIO CANNOT BE HEARD, IS HARD TO HEAR

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the control is operated, the radio comes on, but the sound is poor or crackly.

The defect persists even when tuning into different broadcasters or whilst listening to a tape.

Results of the tests

1

SPEAKER CONNECTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the connections between the speakers and the radio See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections : See E3510 RADIO

2

SPEAKER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speakers.

Continue to Step 3

Front speaker defective

Replace the speaker Op. 5570T90 front speaker - r r

Front tweeter defective

Replace the speaker Op. 5570T92 front tweeter - r r

Rear speaker defective

Replace the speaker Op. 5570T96 rear side speaker - r r

Rear tweeter defective

Replace the speaker Op. 5570T94 rear tweeter (one), lh or rh - r r

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

When the control is operated, the radio comes on, but the sound is poor or crackly.

The defect persists even when tuning into different broadcasters or whilst listening to a tape.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the audio ambient is not OFF. Check that the FADER and the BALANCE have not been moved and placed at zero

Continue to Step 1

AUDIO OFF condition and/or poor balance of the Connect audio channels

Restore the correct values

1

INTERNAL AMPLIFIER OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is not present for other audio sources. (Listen to an audio reference CD in good condition in the Connect player and/or tune into a radio station with good reception in that area))

Continue to Step 2

Poor audio reception from the CD only

Replace the Connect Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

Poor audio reception from the tuner only.

See table N12 POOR RADIO RECEPTION

Poor audio reception for all sound sources.

Continue to Step 3

2

ERRORS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in the 'Reading Parameters' ambient for the Connect Node, check that there are no errors or that the following one or more errors are not present:

  • Front speaker right
  • Front speaker left
  • Rear speaker right
  • Rear speaker left
  • GPS aerial
  • AM/FM aerial
  • Internal error.

Continue to Step 4

Error for right front speaker.

Continue to Step 3

Error for left front speaker.

Continue to Step 3

Error for right rear speaker.

Continue to Step 3

Error for left rear speaker.

Continue to Step 3

GPS aerial error.

See table N36 THE NAVIGATOR IS NOT WORKING

AM/FM aerial error.

See table N12 POOR RADIO RECEPTION

Internal error

Replace the Connect Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

CHECK WITH ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speakers using the Examiner in 'Active Diagnosis' ambient for the Connect Node,

  • Front speaker right
  • Front speaker left
  • Rear speaker right
  • Rear speaker left

Continue to Step 5

Speaker(s) defective

Replace the defective speaker(s)

4

SPEAKER CONNECTIONS CHECK

Connections not correct
