E01 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

There is sticking or noise during low gear changes or when engaging reverse gear

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the engine idling, check whether the fault occurs in all gears

Check the clutch pedal travel

Continue to Step 1

It occurs in all gears

Continue to Step 1

Only when engaging certain gears

See table C02 GEAR ENGAGEMENT OR RELEASE DIFFICULT

1

VISUAL CHECK FOR LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the pipes - clutch

Check that there are no leaks or damage

Check for leaks from the bell housing (clutch actuator area)

Continue to Step 2

Leaks, damage or seepage from the pipes and connectors

Renew the defetive or damaged components

Oil leaks from the hydraulic clutch actuator

Replace clutch operating actuator Op. 1820B38 hydraulic clutch actuator r r and bleed air with gearbox removed

2

CLUTCH PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Press the clutch pedal and check that the travel and force are correct

Continue to Step 3

Force on the pedal slight, the fully reaches the end of travel position without any resistance

Replace the pump Op. 1820B20 (Pump) - MASTER CYLINDER R.R.

3

CHECK FOR AIR IN THE SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Operate the pedal and check whether it is excessively soft

Continue to Step 4

Pedal very soft

Bleed the air Op. 3330A14 HYDRAULIC BRAKING SYSTEM - Bleed air

4

CHECK ON CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visual check on the condition of the clutch assembly, which should not show breaks (pressure plate) and/or obvious deterioration (plate)

Continue to Step 5

Obvious deterioration of the clutch plate; pressure plate broken

Replace the assembly Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

5

CHECK ON CLUTCH SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • that there is no excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft
  • the condition of the main shaft hub

Continue to Step 6

Clutch plate hub worn

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed

Shaft damaged

Replace the main shaft Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

6

FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check in particular:

  • condition of clutch support surface
  • wear, signs of overheating

Continue to Step 7

Flywheel damaged

Replace flywheel Op. 1024B32

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TS; all production periods

There is sticking or noise during low gear changes or when engaging reverse gear

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Road test the vehicle to check the operation of the gearbox, selecting all the gears manually.

Continue to Step 1

The fault is present in all gears

Continue to Step 1

The fault is only present when some gears are engaged

See table C02 GEAR ENGAGEMENT OR RELEASE DIFFICULT

1

VISUAL CHECK FOR LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the electro-hydraulic gear selection/engagement - clutch assembly and the bell housing (clutch actuator area)

Continue to Step 2

Leaks from pipes and hoses

Eliminate leaks and replace defective components following recommended procedures

2

CHECK ON CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visual check on the condition of the clutch assembly, which should not show breaks (pressure plate) and/or obvious deterioration (plate)

Continue to Step 3

Obvious deterioration of the clutch plate; pressure plate broken

Replace the assembly Op. 1810A28 clutch - r r for manual gearbox with hydraulic selection versions

3

CHECK ON CLUTCH SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • the condition of the clutch plate
  • the condition of the main shaft hub
  • In addition to the check described above, check that the thrust bearing control lever rotates freely

Continue to Step 4

Clutch plate hub worn / damaged

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed

Shaft damaged

Replace the main shaft Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

Thrust bearing control lever seized

Replace lever bushes Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK
