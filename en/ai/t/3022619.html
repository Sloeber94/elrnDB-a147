Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the door/s is/are opened (irrespective of whether the ignition is switched ON or OFF) the courtesy lights do not come on.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs Op. 7040E24 lower courtesy light indoor panel - r+r

2

SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the switches in the locks

Continue to Step 3

Switch faulty

Replace the lock Op. 7005N21 front side door lock motor-lock assembley-r r

3

CHECKING COURTESY LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • DRIVER'S DOOR CONNECTOR CONTROL UNIT (services) supply fuse;
  • connections between BODY COMPUTER control unit and door opening switches (locks)
  • connections between NPG CONTROL UNIT AND BODY COMPUTER (LOW SPEED CAN)
  • connections between DRIVER'S DOOR CONNECTOR CONTROL UNIT and courtesy light bulbs;
  • bulb earths : See E2521 COURTESY LIGHTS - Location of components

Continue to Step 4

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse (and search for the cause of its blowing)

Connections broken

Restore the connections : See E2521 COURTESY LIGHTS - Location of components

Bulb earth faulty

Restore the earth contact : See E2521 COURTESY LIGHTS - Location of components

4

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
