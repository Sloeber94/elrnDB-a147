Functional description

Climate control control unit M70 is supplied when the ignition is turned on (INT/A) at pin 25 of a line protected by fuse F31 from underfacia junction unit B2 . ; it is supplied directly at pin 31 from a line protected by fuse F39 of underfacia control unit B2 .

The supply for lighting the controls with the side lights on reaches pin 24 from the line protected by fuse F35 of underfacia control unit B2.

Pin 21 of M70 is earthed.

Control unit M70 is also connected to sensors K87 (right lower treated air - connector B - and left - connector A), K88 (right upper treated air - connector B - and left - connector A); these sensors receive a reference earth from pin 32 and send signals to pins 19, 20, 29 and 30 of M70 .

Passenger comparment air sensor K89 also receives a reference earth from pin 32 and sends a signal to pin 37 of M70 . Sensor K89 is fitted with a fan motor controlled by the ignition so that the temperature reading is not affected by the motionless and thus warmer air inside the dashboard.

The solar ray sensor K90 informs the control unit of the heat coming from rays: it receives a 5V supply from pin 38 control unit M70 and gives out two signals: to pin 39 for the right area and to pin 40 for the left area.

The exhaust gas presence sensor (A.Q.S.) K64 supplies a recirculation control signal when the air pollution exceeds pre-set levels: it is supplied at pin 1 from the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by fuse F31 of underfacia control unit B2 . pin 2 is earthed and pin 3 sends a signal to pin 27 of M70 .

Demisting sensor K03 is supplied at pin 2 of ignition-operated line (INT) protected by fuse F49 of underfacia control unit B2 ; Pin 1 is earthed and pin 3 sends a signal to pin 28 of M70 .

The air conditioning system is equipped with one recirculation actuator ( N82 ), two air blending actuators (N78 and N79) and two air districution actuators ( N81 and N83 ).

Recirculation actuator N82 is controlled by control unit M70 , via pins 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Mixing actuators N78 and N79 are controlled from control unit M70 via pins 5, 6, 7, 8 and 11, 12, 13 14 respectively.

Distribution actuator N80 is controlled from control unit M70 from pins 15, 16, 17 and 18.

An electronic governer M72 controls fan N85 at different speeds via an earth command: this is ignition-operated (INT/A) from a line protected by fuse F31 of underfacia control unit B2 . It receives a control signal from pin 33 of the control unit and returns a feedback signal to pin 9 of M70 .

Fan N85 is supplied directly from a line protected by fuse F5 of engine bay control unit B1 .

Pin 36 of M70 controls activation of compressor E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT .

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: Condizionatore automatico

Functional description

The outdoor temperature sensor is located on door mirror P60 : the sensor is connected to the control unit on door H44 and thence, via the CAN network, to M70 .

Pins 34 and 35 of control unit M70 connect to Body Computer M1 and node H44 via the CAN line and exchange the following signals:

  • speedometer signal
  • windscreen washer activation signal for recirculation activation
  • window and sun-roof closure control
  • battery voltage signal
  • rear window activation signal
