Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The sunroof control motor N35 is supplied at pin 7 by the line protected by fuse F63 of the fuse box B98 , via the direct line protected by fuse F2 of B1 .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Functional description

The sunroof control motor N35 is supplied at pin 7 by the line protected by fuse F63 of the fuse box B98, via the direct line protected by fuse CPL2 of B5.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

Pin 8 of N35 receives the automatic roof closure signal, in acordance with the aobve logic, coming from pin 10 of connector E of the Body Computer M1

Pin 9 of N35 receives the speedometer signal from pin 23 of connector C of the Body Computer M1 .

Pin1 of N35 supplies the switch H60 , which sends the various control signals, corresponding to the various knob positions, to pins 2, 3, 4 and 5 of N35 .

The aerial-receivier P93 sends the Body Computer M1 the signal to open/close the doors and also windows and sunroof, by connecting to pins 21 and 31 of connector A.
