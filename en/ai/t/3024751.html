Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with the removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Left side).

Proceed with the removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. (Left side).

Position a hydraulic jack under the gearbox.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit gearbox side rigid support to the flexible mounting.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and (2b) and remov the power unit gearbox side rigid support (2c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the gearbox side power unit rigid support (1a) in its seating and secure to the gearbox by tightening screws (1b) and bolts (1c) to the specified torque.
