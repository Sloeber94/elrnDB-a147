Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The instruments light up when the ignition is turned on. In the START position the starter motor does not turn

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING STARTER MOTOR POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the supply lead between battery and starter motor is undamaged and properly secured

Continue to Step 2

loose fastenings on lead

Secure lead

2

CHECKING STARTER MOTOR RELAY POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the connection between switch and relay

Continue to Step 3

Lack of continuity

Use a tester to check if the lack of continuity is between switch and engine bay control unit, or in the engine bay between engine bay control unit and starter motor relay. See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Lack of continuity in the control unit.

If the lack of continuity is in the control unit Op. 5505A13 supplementary junction control unit in engine bay - r r, replace the control unit. Otherwise repair the lead in question.

3

CHECKING SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the switch is working and undamaged. Operate the switch

Continue to Step 4

Lack of contact at pin 50

Replace switch Op. 5520A12 ignition switch assembly - r r for versions with air bag

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists
