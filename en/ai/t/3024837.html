Refitting

1. Remove the clips (1a) and remove the injectors (1b) from the single fuel manifold pipe.

2. Remove the seals from the injectors.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit new seals on the injectors.

Refit the injectors into their housings on the single fuel manifold pipe and secure with clips.

Refitting

Removing

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the retainers (1a) and remove the fuel injectors (1b) from the fuel manifold pipe.

2. Remove the O-rings from the injectors.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the new O-rings in the seats on the injectors.

Place the injectors in their seats on the fuel manifold pipe and secure them using the safety clips.
