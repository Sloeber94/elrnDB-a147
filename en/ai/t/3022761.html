All OK

Problems met

Operation

That that the following are absent:

  • oily dross on the valves;
  • defects in the cylinder block/crankcase plane;
  • defects in the cylinder head gasket;

CYLINDER LINER/BORE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cylinder liners/bores. In particular:

  • no vitrification of the liners/bores;
  • make sure that there is no ovality or wear

Deep scoring or excessive ovality
