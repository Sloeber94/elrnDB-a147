When the control is operated, the radio does not come on.

Results of the tests

1

RADIO CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the protective fuse behind the actual radio : Characteristic of working principle 5570 ACCESSORIES

Continue to Step 2

Fuse faulty

Replace the fuse and search for the cause of the problem

2

RADIO ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Radio protective fuse;
  • Radio supply
  • Radio earth
  • Radio electrical circuit continuity and insulation
  • See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the fuse and search for the cause of the problem

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E3510 RADIO
