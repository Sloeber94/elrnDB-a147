The radio comes on normally, but the station reception is not excellent.

The conditions in which the radio is tested should allow excellent reception: check that therre are no tall buildings or trees which could impair reception

Results of the tests

0

RECEPTION ZONE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the problem is not caused by an unsuitable choice of filters (e.g. AF, PTY, TA, etc.) or occurs in poor reception areas, when moving away from these areas the reception should improve considerably. It is also possible, if the reception area is very bad, that attempts to switch between frequencies may seem like pauses.

Continue to Step 1

Poor or no reception due to unsuitable choice of filters.

Deactivate the selection involved.
