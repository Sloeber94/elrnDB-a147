CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the following in order at the disconnected CD-Changer connector:

  • Presence of signals enabling reading of music discs. See E3510 RADIO See Test 5580PH Check on operation of CD changer and radio module

No signals enabling reading of music discs in the CD-Changer.

Replace the Connect
