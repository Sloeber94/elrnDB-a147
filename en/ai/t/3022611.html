H10 STEERING HARD / (STEERING IN BOTH DIRECTIONS)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During normal operation of the steering there is a stiffening of the steering when steering to both the right and the left.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the tyre pressures are correct

Check that the power steering fluid level is correct

Continue to Step 1

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the correct pressures

Power assisted steering fluid level too low

Restore the correct level: if there are leaks

See table X30 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

1

CHECK CONDITION OF STEERING GAITERS AND TIE-RODS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the steering gaiters and tie-rods

Continue to Step 2

Steering tie-rods damaged or worn

Replace the tie-rods Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

2

CHECK TYPE OF FAULT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a test, turning the steering wheel to full lock under various conditions, checking that there are no faults.

Stiffness continuous and constant

Continue to Step 3

Hardness intermittent

Check that the filter inside the reservoir is correctly positioned. if necessary, replace the reservoir Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

Hardness only during idling

Check for any restrictions in the pipes (flexible and rigid). If necessary replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals

Hardness in end of travel position

Restore the correct wheel geometry

3

STEERING COLUMN BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and efficiency of the steering column support bearings, disconnecting the column from the steering box

Continue to Step 4

Bearings worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved Op. 4110A31

4

CHECK OPERATION OF STEERING BOX

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the front wheels lifted, make the rack complete its full travel in both directions, making sure that there is no excessive stiffness or play.

Continue to Step 5

Faulty operation of steering box

Replacing steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

5

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the wheels on the ground, check the correct operation of the power steering pump

Continue to Step 6

Belt slack

Check and, if necessary, replace the belt Op. 1092C14

Power assisted steering pump malfunction

Replace the power assisted steering pump Op. 4130B20 power steering pump r r

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
