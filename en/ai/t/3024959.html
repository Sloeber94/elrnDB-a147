Removing

Refitting

.

The manual gearbox cannot be removed-refitted with the differential in the vehicle.

The power unit must be removed-refitted and then the manual gearbox and differential separated from the engine at the bench.

Drain the air conditioner coolant circuit 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling .

Proceed with removal 1048A48 throttle heater sleeve - r r .

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace .

Proceed with removal 4450B12 front or rear wheels (two) - r+r .

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with the removal 5540B76 Front LIGHT CLUSTER with fog light (one), left or right - R.R. with interfering components removed (Right and left sides).

Proceed with removal 7025A34 bonnet lid lock (one) - r+r or repair .

Proceed with removal 7210A15 R.R. HEADLAMP HOUSING/FRONT WING CROSSMEMBER .

Proceed with removal 1088B20 engine cooling radiator - r + r .

Proceed with removal 1076B82 left exhasust pipe between catalytic preconverter and converter r r .

Proceed with removal 1008A44 rear rigid mount - r r .

Proceed with removal 1004B10 power unit with manual gearbox - r + r .

Proceed with removal 1004D10 power unit (manual gearbox), removed, remove and re-build .

Proceed with removal 2110A10 manual gearbox and differential - r + r .

Proceed with removal 1810A24 clutch - r + r with gearbox removed .

Proceed with removal 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed .

Proceed with removal 1092G10 single engine belt r r .

Proceed with removal 5510C34 rpm and timing sensor (single) - r+r .

Proceed with the removal 1020A20 crank case oil sump - r + r clean seals for sealant application

Proceed with removal 1020D50 crank shaft rear mount - apply silicone to mating surfaces with crank case sump and flywheel removed .
