Refitting

7040E50 front modular courtesy light unit - r+r

7065C33 sun blind with courtesy mirror and courtesy light one lh or rh r r (left and right sides)

1. Undo the front screws securing the ceiling trim.

2. Slightly lower the ceiling trim.

3. Undo the screws fixing the rain sensor unit.

Once removed, the rain sensor can no longer be reused and must be replaced.

4. Move the rain sensor unit slightly.

5. Disconnect the electrical connection and recover the sensor.

Refitting

Removing

5050B64 rain sensor electronic unit r r .

Thoroughly clean the inside of the windscreen by the sensor housing. Use a degreasing product.

Connect the sensor electrical connection.

Remove the protection from the jelly-like surface of the new sensor, then place it in position.

During this stage, take care not to touch the jelly-like surface with your hands.
