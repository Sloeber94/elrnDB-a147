A leak of engine coolant is seen with dripping and/or fouling of the engine

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily carry out a visual inspect to locate the component or area which is leaking.

Check that there is no engine oil in the coolant.

Check that there is no water or emulsion in the engine oil.

If the visual inspection is insufficient, wash the engine, dry it and let it slowly reache operating temperature: to identify the leak, if necessary, carry out the system leakage test Op. 0510C30 cooling system - tightness test

CYLINDER BLOCK/CRANKCASE PLANE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the cylinder head Op. 1016C10 cylinder head - r + r and replace gasket

That that the following are absent:

  • oily dross on the valves;
  • defects in the cylinder block/crankcase plane;
  • defects in the cylinder head gasket;

Continue to Step 9

Cylinder block/crankcase plane defective

Reface the cylinder block/crankcase

Presence of oily dross on the valves

Overhaul the cylinder head Op. 1016E10 single cylinder head, removed - overhaul
