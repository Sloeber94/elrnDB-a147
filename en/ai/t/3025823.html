Fold back the front seat squabs fully.

1. Undo the front screws securing the ceiling trim.

2. Remove the door seals near the ceiling trim.

3. Release the inner Velcro retainers.

4. Remove the ceiling trim and withdraw it from the interior compartment through the front right door.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trim is undamaged.

Insert the ceiling trim into the interior compartment through the front right door.

Position the ceiling trim and engage the inner Velcro retainers.

Tighten the front screws securing the ceiling trim.

Fit the door compartment seals.

Raise the front seat squabs.
