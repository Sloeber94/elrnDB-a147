1008B10 power unit front mount (timing side) flexible block - replace

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with the removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

Position a hydraulic jack under the engine.

1. Undo the nut securing timing side rigid engine support to the flexible mounting to the recommended torque.

Lower the hydraulic jack as necessary.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the flexible mounting for the power unit front support (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the flexible mounting for the power unit front support (1a) back in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolts (1b) to the recommended torque.
