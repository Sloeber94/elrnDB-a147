Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the control is operated, the sun-roof opens/closes in an irregular manner or it remains locked.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • that the glass is correctly aligned with the roof profile
  • that there are no foreign bodies or dirt along the runners and guides.

Continue to Step 1

Alignment not correct

Align the glass with the roof profile Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r

Presence of dirt or foreign bodies along the runners or guides

Restore the correct operation of the runners and guides.

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the sun roof assembly and check:

  • That the stop screws for the drive cables are fitted
  • Wear of drive cables
  • Runner pads broken or worn
  • Runners broken or worn
  • Runner guides broken

Continue to Step 2

Bolts missing

Restore the bolts

Cables, runners or guide pads broken or worn

Replace the damaged components Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

Runner guides broken or worn

Replace the sun roof assembly Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2
