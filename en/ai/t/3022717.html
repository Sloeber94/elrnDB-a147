Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is started, the Passenger Air Bag Disablement warning light comes on for 4 seconds, goes off and then flashes continuously

The flashing of the 'Passenger Air Bag disabled' warning light indicates a fault on the AIR BAG warning light control circuit.
