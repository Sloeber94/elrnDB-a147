With the key in the ON position and the side lights on, the instrument panel does not light up

the side lights are working properly

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check the operatio of the instrument panel light in NQS active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 2

Instrument panel does not light up

Panel defective; Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

All OK

Operation

Check the supplies for the instrument panel and its connection with the Body Computer : See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

Continue to Step 3

Connections / supplies not correct

Restore correct connections / supplies : See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING
