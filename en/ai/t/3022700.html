With panel on, seat heat pads do not work.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the heat pads have been deactivated by means of the switches at either side of the seats.

Device operation is automatically shut off at a certain temperature by a thermal contact located inside the device

Continue to Step 1

Switch in OFF position

Activate the switch

Switch in ON position

Continue to Step 1

1

CHECKING HEAT PAD POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the heat pad connectors are properly supplied with power See E3075 HEATED SEATS

Continue to Step 2

No supply

Check the condition of the specific facia junction unit fuse, and the continuity of leads from the control unit to the heat pads: See E3075 HEATED SEATS

2

CHECKING SWITCH OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Disconnect the switches and check their efficiency using a Tester

Continue to Step 3

Switches not working properly

Replace switches Op. 7045A43 seat exterior guard - r r

CHECKING HEAT PAD POWER SUPPLY

Problems met

Operation

Remove the seat trim and check the heat pads are in good condition

Cushion heat pad damaged

Replace the heat pad Op. 7045M22 l/h or r/h front seat cushion heat pad - r+r following electrical wiring check

Squab heat pad damaged
