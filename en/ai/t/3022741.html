Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion with the sun-roof closed, a noise is heard coming from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof closed

Continue to Step 1

The noise occurs with the roof open

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

The noise occurs with the roof tilted open

See table S77 SUN ROOF NOISY / WTH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • Roof panel to runners;
  • Complete roof mechanism to the bodyshell

Continue to Step 2

Panel fastening faulty

Re-attach correctly Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r

Complete mechanism fixing to bodyshell

Restore the correct fastening Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove glass [PR7030B18] and check operation of the sun-roof operating devices:

  • Check the felt pads are present on the central beam
  • Check the internal trim control springs are present and properly fastened
  • Check that the Teflon mount between slides and rails is present
  • Check that the felt pads between spoiler and front channel are present

Continue to Step 3

Sun roof fastenings and/or operating devices faulty

Restore the faulty components and the entire mechanism fixing Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
