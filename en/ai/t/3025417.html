Refitting

Removing

Check that the drier filter is intact and there are no signs of damage.

If removed, position the mount and fix it using the nuts.

Follow these precautions:

Do not leave the filter with the connectors disconnected for longer than is strictly necessary.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors.

- Use only green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

- Lubricate the threads of the connectors using anti-freeze.

1. Connect the pipe connectors without tightening the fixing bolts.

2. Position the drier filter.

3. Position the bracket.

4. Tighten the bolt.

5. Tighten the bolts fixing the connectors to the recommended torque.
