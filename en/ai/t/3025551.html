1. Release the retainers (1a) and remove the cover (1b).

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

3. Release the retaining spring moving it sideways.

4. Extract the bulb from the lamp.

If the lamp is working properly, avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.
