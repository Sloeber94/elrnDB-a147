Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When one of the doors is opened, this is not displayed by the special warning light/icon in the instrument panel. When the doors are opened, the interior lights work properly.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer;

Continue to Step 2

There are faults in the Body Computer

Proceed in accordance with the instructions provided by the instrument

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket.

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the instrument panel

Continue to Step 3

There are faults in the instrument panel

Proceed in accordance with the instructions provided by the instrument

3

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket

Carry out the active diagnosis of the warning lights

Continue to Step 4

Warning light not working

Display/instrument panel defective Replace the instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

4

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check connections between:

  • Body Computer and switches in locks

Continue to Step 5

Connections not correct

Restore the correct connections : See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

5

SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the switches in the locks

Continue to Step 6

Lock with switch defective

Replace the lock Op. 7005N21 front side door lock motor-lock assembley-r r

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Faulty Body Computer

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r
