Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and the nut (1b) and remove the lower reinforcement link (1c) for the power unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the power unit lower reaction rod (1a) back in its housing and fix it tightening the bolt (1b) and the nut (1c) to the recommended torque.
