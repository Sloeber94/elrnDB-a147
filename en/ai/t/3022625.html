Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, the Body Computer does not detect faults in the operation of the side lights, stop lights or rear fog lamps, despite the fact that they are not working, and the 'Light failure' warning light does not come on

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check light operation on the instrument panel while in active diagnosis mode

Continue to Step 2

Light failure warning light does not come on

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

Light failure warning light comes on

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK ON BODY COMPUTER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • connections between Body Computer and instrument panel
  • CONNECTIONS BETWEEN BODY COMPUTER AND BULBS
  • Body Computer power supply: See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections: See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Incorrect supply

Restore the correct supply See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
