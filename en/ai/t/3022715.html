Applies to: all engines; all production periods

On starting, the 'Passenger Air Bag Disabled' warning light comes on and remains on for longer than 4 seconds (control unit initial test)

With the Passenger Bag exclusion switch in the OFF position the warning light is lit, while in the ON position the light is not lit.

Results of the tests

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • That the passenger Air Bag disabling switch is in the ON position.
