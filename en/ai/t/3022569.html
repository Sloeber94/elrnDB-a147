Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Smooth gear engagement without grating is difficult in all conditions.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON FIXING OF GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fastenings of the gearbox control cables: they should not be slack or incorrectly fixed

Continue to Step 2

Cables not fastened correctly

Restore the correct fastening of the gearbox control cables Op. 2125C10

2

CHECK ON GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the gearbox flexible cables and that they slide correctly

Continue to Step 3

Gearbox flexible cables faulty

Replace the gearbox control cables Op. 2125C20

3

GEAR LEVER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the correct operation of the gear lever ball joint

Continue to Step 4

Gear lever ball joint faulty

Replace the gear lever Op. 2125C24 gear lever knob - r + r
