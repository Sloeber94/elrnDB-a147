1008A44 rear rigid mount - r r

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the gearbox.

2. Undo the nut fixing the reinforcement rod to the engine block.

3. Undo the bolt fixing the power unit rigid rear support to the cylinder block/crankcase.

4. Undo the bolt (4a) and the nut (4b) fixing the rigid rear support for the power unit to the gearbox.

5. Loosen the bolt fixing the reinforcement rod to the power unit rigid rear support.

6. Undo the bolt (6a) and remove the power unit rigid rear support (6b) complete with reaction rod (6c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the power unit rigid rear suport (1a), complete with reinforcement rod (1b) back in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolt (1c) to the flexible mounting to the recommended torque.
