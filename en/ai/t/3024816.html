1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When scrapping the submerged pump assembly with level gauge mechanism, always empty of fuel as follows:While removing the submerged pump assembly, draw off any fuel in the tank using an in-line pump (electric or manual);fully remove the submerged pump assembly then, working at the bench, connect an in-line pump (electric or manual) to the assembly connector;Run the pump for as long enough to collect most of the petrol present inside the filter.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods
