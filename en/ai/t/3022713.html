Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During starting, the 'Air Bag failure' warning light comes on and remains on for more than 4 seconds (control unit initial test)

9

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Before any R/R operation involving the explosive charges, disconnect the battery leads for 10 minutes

Check the connection between the Air Bag control unit and the Air Bag failure warning light at the instrument panel connector.

To switch off the fault warning light, the AIR BAG control unit sends a posistive signal to the instrument panel.

Continue to Step 10

Faulty wiring

Replace wiring: See E7030 AIRBAG
