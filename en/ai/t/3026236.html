Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Functional description

Relay T5 of junction unit B1B controls the compressor engagement circuit.

The relay supply arrives from the line of maxifuse JB/3 of B5 via the line for fuse F1 of junction unit B1A.

The supply for the coil for relay T5 arrives from the main injection relay T9 located in junction unit B1B See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT .

Climate control system ECU M70 sends an enablement signal for compressor activation to 4-stage pressure switch K10: if pressures in the coolant circuit are correct, the signal emerges from the pressure switch and reaches engine control ECU M10, pin 6 of connector A. According to its inner control strategy, control unit M10 send a compressor activation command from pin 13 of connector A which excites relay T5 so that the electromagnet for activation of air conditioner compressor L20 is controlled at 12 V.
