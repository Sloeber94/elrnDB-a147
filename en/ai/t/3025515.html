Removing

Refitting

1. If necessary, regulate the steering wheel height adjustment lever.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the lower trim.

3. Remove the lower trim, releasing it from the inner retainers and the steering wheel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connections (1a, 1b) from the ignition switch.

2. Release the retainers (2a) and remove the contact holder connector (2b) from its housing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the contact holder connector is not damaged.

Position the contact holder connector and attach it to the retainers.

Connect the electrical connection.

Refit the lower half casing and secure it using the bolts.

Check the operation of the ignition switch.
