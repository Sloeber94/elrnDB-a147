S62 FRONT DOORS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from one of the front doors

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING DASHBOARD/DOOR PANEL MATCHING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door panel and facia fit together properly

Continue to Step 2

Dashboard fixing faulty

Repair the facia fastening

2

CHECKING DOOR FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the hinges (upper and lower)
  • Of the lock

Continue to Step 3

Hinge fixing faulty

If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin. Repair the fastening Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

Lock fixing faulty

Refit the lock correctly Op. 7005N21 front side door lock motor-lock assembley-r r

Striker faulty

Refix the striker correctly Op. 7005B14 front side door catch striker r r

3

CHECKING DOOR PANEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the door panel Op. 7005E08 front side door interior panel r rand check for foreign bodies inside the door

Continue to Step 4

Presence of foreign bodies

Remove foreign bodies

4

CHECKING INTERNAL DOOR FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the window opening;
  • Of moving window glass guides;
  • Of the element fixing the moving window;
  • Of the door opening/closing linkage

Continue to Step 5

Window fastening faulty

Repair window fastening Op. 7005M21 front side door electrically operated window - r r

Front guide fastening faulty

Refit the guides correctly Op. 7005D21 front door window front guide - r r

Rear guide fastening faulty

Refit the guides correctly Op. 7005D24 front door window rear guide - r r

Fastening element faulty

Replace the fastening element Op. 7005D16 fixed window glass element - r r

Linkage fixing faulty

Renew the linkage fastening springs Op. 7005C54 front side door internal opening return mechanism (with handle) - r r for repair or replace with different version

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
