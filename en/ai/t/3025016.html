Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the connection (1a) and undo connection (1b), with integrated adjustment valve, securing fuel filter supply pipe to the fuel return manifold pipe

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Tighten the connection, with integrated adjustment valve, securing fuel filter supply pipe to the fuel return manifold pipe

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

1. Disconnect the rapid attachment on the fuel return pipe to the fuel return manifold pipe, fuel filter side.

2. Undo the connector with the fuel supply/return adjustment valve incorporated.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Tighten the connector with the fuel supply/return adjustment valve incorporated.

Connect the rapid attachment of the front fuel return pipe to the fuel return manifold pipe, fuel filter side.

Proceed with refitting 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

Proceed with refitting 7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R. .
