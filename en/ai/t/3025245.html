Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. .

Remove the protective cap on the brake caliper bleed valve.

Connect the collection system (pipe and reservoir) to the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Operate the brake pedal until the brake-clutch fluid reservoir is empty.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air flow meter.

Name

Connector

1

Air flow meter

K41

2. Disconnect the oil vapour recovery pipe from the air intake hose.

3. Slacken the bands (3a) and remove the duct from the filter to the throttle body with integrated D.V.L. (3b) complete with air flow meter (3c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the A.B.S. hydraulic and electronic control unit.

Name

Connector

1

ABS control unit

M50

2. Undo the connectors (2a) and (2b) and remove the rigid pipes from the master cylinder to the ABS hydraulic unit (2c).

3. Undo the connectors (3a) and disconnect the rigid pipes from the hydraulic control unit to the brake calipers (3b), hydraulic unit side.

4. Release the ABS pipes from the attachments.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Remove the stops (1a) and remove the hydraulic and electronic unit of the ABS system (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

.

The letters indicating to which caliper and to which brake pump outlet they should be connected are stamped on the control unit at each outlet.

RR = to the right rear brake (pipe marked with a light blue coloured band)

LR = to the left rear brake (pipe marked with a green coloured band)

RF = to the right front caliper (pipe marked with a yellow coloured band)

LF = to the left front caliper (pipe marked with a white coloured band)

MC1 = from the main circuit/flange side pump (pipe marked with a black or pink coloured band when the pipe polyamide resin is black)

MC2 = from the secondary circuit/front pump (pipe marked with a brown coloured band).

Place the ABS hydraulic and electronic unit back in its housing and secure it with its attachments.

1. Connect the rigid pipes from the hydraulic unit to the brake calipers (1a), hydraulic unit side, and tighten the connectors (1b) to the specified torque.
