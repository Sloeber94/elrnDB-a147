LEAKS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Thoroughly wash the gearbox, cover it with talc and road test the vehicle.

Visually check that there are no leaks

In particular, check the condition of the gaskets:

  • gearbox input shaft gasket
  • rear cover gasket
  • differential oil seal

Continue to Step 2

Leaks from the seals

Replace the gaskets Op. 2110C24 seals for gearbox input shaft - r r for manual gearbox with hydraulic selection versions

Leaks from the seals

Replace the flange Op. 2110D53 left hand flange on differential - r+r

Leaks from the seals

Replace the oil seal Op. 2110D62 rh differential shaft oil seal - r r with intermediate drive shaft removed
