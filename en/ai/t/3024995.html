1084C34 engine oil cooling radiator - r + r

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with removal 1084C40 Air DUCT for engine oil cooling radiator - R.R . .

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and disconnect the engine oil inlet and outlet pipes (1b), engine oil cooling radiator side.

Collect the engine oil in a suitable container.

2. Undo the nuts (2a) and remove the engine oil cooling radiator (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the engine oil cooling radiator back in its housing and fix it using the nuts.

Connect the engine oil inlet and outlet pipes, engine oil cooling radiator side and tighten the bolts.

Proceed with refitting 1084C40 Air DUCT for engine oil cooling radiator - R.R . .

Proceed with refitting 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with refitting 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system 0010T11 engine oil check level and top up .

Remove the car from the ramps.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with removal 1084C40 Air DUCT for engine oil cooling radiator - R.R . .

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the cooling radiator engine oil supply pipe (1b), radiator side.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and disconnect the cooling radiator engine oil return pipe (2b), radiator side.

Recover the engine oil which comes out in a suitable container.

3. Undo the nuts (3a) and remove the engine cooling radiator (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the engine oil cooling radiator back in its housing and fix it using the nuts.

Connect the engine oil return pipe from the cooling radiator, radiator side, and tighten the bolt.

Connect the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator, radiator side, and tighten the bolt

Proceed with refitting 1084C40 Air DUCT for engine oil cooling radiator - R.R . .

Proceed with refitting 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with refitting 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system 0010T11 engine oil check level and top up .

Remove the car from the ramps.
