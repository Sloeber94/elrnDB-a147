Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is pressed, stiffness is felt during the pedal travel.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Resting the foot on the brake pedal, check that the pedal sinks when the engine is started, because of the vacuum in the braking circuit

Continue to Step 1

Absence of or partial servo assistance

Continue to Step 1

1

CHECK ON VACUUM PIPE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check vacuum pipe: must not be bent or damaged

Continue to Step 2

Pipe bent

Restore the correct geometry

Pipe damaged

Replace the damaged pipe Op. 3330D26 Vacuum PIPE for brake servo assembly - R.R.

2

CHECK NON-RETURN VALVE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the non-return valve is working properly

Continue to Step 3

Faulty operation

Replace check valve Op. 3330D26 Vacuum PIPE for brake servo assembly - R.R.

3

CHECK BRAKE SERVO

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check in particular:

  • that there are no air leaks
  • that there is no seizing or sticking

Continue to Step 4

Brake servo with leaks

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op. 3330D02 brake servo/brake pump assembly - r r

Brake servo operation is jerky

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op. 3330D02 brake servo/brake pump assembly - r r

4

CHECK ON VACUUM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the vacuum level in the servo brake system

Under particular conditions of use, low engine rpm with excessive use of the brakes, the vacuum for operating the servo brake may be insufficient resulting in hardening of the brake pedal. It is pointless replacing the servo unit.

Continue to Step 5

Value incorrect

Eliminate blockages in the manifold

Value correct

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op. 3330D02 brake servo/brake pump assembly - r r

5

CHECKING PUMP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pump has not seized

Check that one or more wheels do not remain braked

Continue to Step 6

Seizure of the pump

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op. 3330D02 brake servo/brake pump assembly - r r

Seizure of a front caliper

Replace the caliper Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

Seizure of a rear caliper

Replace the caliper Op. 3310B36 rear brake calliper - r r

The car remains braked

Remove the servo/pump assembly Op. 3330D02 brake servo/brake pump assembly - r rand adjust the projection of the pushrod

The car remains braked at the rear

Repair the handbrake

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle before delivery to the customer, to check that the fault is no longer present.

The test is essential, as the braking system involves a safety feature of the car

Fault persists
