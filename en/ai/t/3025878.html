Removing

Refitting

7040B10 load platform (luggage platform) trim - r+r

Lift the rear seat cushions.

1. Working from the luggage compartment, undo the squab bracket retaining bolts.

2. Undo the bolts securing the squab to the floor.

Use hexagonal splined wrench N. 7 (USAG 233 S or equivalent).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Only for right squab Undo the bolt and remove the end of the seat belt.

2. Undo the bolt securing the squab to the floor.

Use hexagonal splined wrench N. 7 (USAG 233 S or equivalent).

3. Remove the squab.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the squab is not damaged.

Position the squab.

1. Do up bolts (1a) and (1b) securing the squab to the floor.

Front bolt (1a) must be as specified by the manufacturer because a longer bolt could interfere with fuel tank (1c).

2. Tighten the squab bracket bolts.

3. Tighten the bolts securing the squab to the floor.
