When the windscreen wash/wipe is operated, the smell of the washer fluid enters the passenger compartment (failed automatic operation of the recirculation flaps)

RECIRCULATION MOTOR OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the recirculation motor is working properly (with the ignition ON and the climate control OFF, operate the button)

Continue to Step 2

Continue to Step 2

Operation

Error on CAN line

Continue, following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

3
