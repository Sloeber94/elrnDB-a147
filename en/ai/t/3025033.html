Removing

Refitting

1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r

Release the return manifold from the intake manifold.

1. Unscrew the pipe connector from the return manifold.

2. Unscrew the pipe connector from the pressure pump.

3. Remove the pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit a new pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold.

Whenever you work on the pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold, always fit a new pipe when reassembling to avoid leakage from the fittings.

Tighten the connectors on the pressure pump and return manifold to torque.

Refitting

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

1. Undo the connectors (1a), (1b) and remove the pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold (1c).

When refitting, fit a new pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the pipe from the pressure pump to the single fuel manifold back in its housing and fix it using the connectors without tightening them.

2. Tighten the connector for the pipe from the pressure pump to the single fuel manifold, pump side, to the recommended torque using a suitable spanner.
