5580CA Driver's Air Bag check

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Disconnect the Bag connector at the line concerned and connect the special line closure resistor.

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

Cancel the errors and check that they have disappeared.

If the error persists, disconnect the connector for the clock spring and connect the special liner closure resistor.

Cancel the errors and check that they have disappeared.

Reconnect the Bag and clock spring connectors.
