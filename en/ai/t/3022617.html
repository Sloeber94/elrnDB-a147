I10 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS / NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, when the steering column switch unit lever is raised or lowered, the direction indicators do not work. When the button is pressed, the hazard warning lights do not work

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer
  • through the active diagnosis, the operation of the lights.

Continue to Step 3

Direction indicators error. Individual bulb not working (not all)

Check individual lights.

Continue to Step 2

All the bulbs are not working

Check lights electric circuit.

Continue to Step 5

2

CHECKING INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Front bulb damaged

Change the bulb Op. 5550C30 l/h or r/h front turn signal (one) - r+r

Damaged rear bulb

Change the bulb Op. 5540A56 dipped/main beam electrical wiring - check component operation, line continuity and repair contacts - includes relay replacement if necessary

Side repeater damaged

Replace the side repeater Op. 5550C22 l/h or r/h side flasher (one) - r+r

Rear bulb holder assembly faulty

Replace the bulb holder assembly Op. 5550C30 l/h or r/h front turn signal (one) - r+r

Front bulb holder assembly faulty

Replace the bulb holder assembly Op. 5540A56 dipped/main beam electrical wiring - check component operation, line continuity and repair contacts - includes relay replacement if necessary

3

CHECKING STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly and check that the direction indicators switch is working properly Characteristic of working principle 5550 MANOEUVRING AND WARNING SIGNALS

Continue to Step 4

Steering column switch unit not working properly

Replace the stalk unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

4

CHECK HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the hazard warning lights switch: See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description

Continue to Step 5

Hazard warning lights switch faulty

Replace hazard warning lights switch Op. 5505A24 w/l and service switch mount - r+r

5

CHECK DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between stalk unit and Body Computer
  • connections between CENTRE CONTROL PANEL and Body Computer;
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs;
  • earth and condition of bulbs: See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description

Continue to Step 6

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections: See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description

6

CHECK ON BODY COMPUTER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Body Computer is supplied correctly

Continue to Step 7

Incorrect supply

Restore the correct supply See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description

Body Computer faulty

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
