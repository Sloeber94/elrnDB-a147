Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The interior lights do not work when the relevant controls are operated (including the boot light).

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • condition of individual bulbs
  • condition of the relevant bulb holders
  • condition and operation of courtesy light switches.

Continue to Step 2

Faulty bulb or front courtesy light

Restore or replace the courtesy light Op. 7040E40 modular front courtesy light - r r

Faulty bulb or rear courtesy light.

Restore or replace the courtesy light Op. 7040E16 rear courtesy light - r+r

Faulty boot bulb or bulb holder

Restore or replace the bulb holder Op. 7040G30 boot light bulb holder - r r

2

CHECK ON INTERIOR LIGHTING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Body Computer supply;
  • connections between courtesy lights and Body Computer;
  • connection to +30 courtesy lights;
  • courtesy light earth connection: See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS ,

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore correct connections See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Body Computer earth faulty

Restore earth connection: See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Faulty Body Computer supply

Restore connection: See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

3

CHECK ON SIGNAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that when one of the side doors is opened, an earth signal is sent from the Body Computer to the courtesy light

Continue to Step 4

No earth signal from Body Computer

Restore the correct supply to the Body Computer. If necessary, replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
