S65 SEATS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from one of the front doors

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING GUARD AND CONTROL FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • Of guards;
  • Of seat belt anchorage.

Continue to Step 2

Fault in front guard fastenings

Refasten correctly, and refit felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A43 seat exterior guard - r r

Fault in rear guard fastenings

Refasten correctly, and refit felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A44 seat interior guard - r r

Seat belt fastening fault

Restore the correct fastening Op. 7065A12 front seat belt (one), lh or rh, with pretensioner on wheel - r r

Noise noted during simulation

Eliminate the interference on the seat lift links

2

CHECK ON SEAT SLIDE DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seat runners are properly fastened

  • To the car floor pan
  • To the actual seat.

Also check the guide release bow is sliding efficiently

Check the bow engagement notches

Continue to Step 3

Device fastening faulty

Reattach the seat runners correctly

Sliding uneven

Replace the guides Op. 7045A71 front seat support/guide - driver`s side with lift device - r r

Bow deformation

Replace the deformed bow

Engagement notches worn

Replace the guides Op. 7045A71 front seat support/guide - driver`s side with lift device - r r

3

CHECKING SEAT LIFT DEVICE (driver's side only)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seat lifting device In particular, check that the clearance is not excessive

Also try to simulate movement during cornering to ensure no fouling is present.

The seat will lift only it someone is sitting in it

Continue to Step 4

Clearance excessive

Replace the seat lift device and fastenings Op. 7045A45

Fouling

Move fouling parts apart

4

CHECK LUMBAR ADJUSTMENT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the lumbar adjustment device In particular, check that the clearance is not excessive

Continue to Step 5

Clearance excessive

Change the lumbar adjustment device Op. 7045A38 device for driver`s seat squab lumbar adjustment - r r with seat removed

5

CHECK SEAT SLIDE RELEASE LEVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the release lever for longitudinal adjustment of the seat.

In particular, check that the clearance is not excessive

Continue to Step 6

Clearance excessive

Replace the release lever Op. 7045A71 front seat support/guide - driver`s side with lift device - r r

6

CHECK FOR FOULING BENEATH GUIDES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove seat Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+rand check for fouling against carpet or sound-proofing beneath the guides

Continue to Step 7

Fouling present

Remove the fouling and refit the seat correctly Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r

7

CHECK LUMBAR ADJUSTMENT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove part of seat upholstery Op. 7045A92 Front seat COVER, left or right - R.R. with seat removed

Verify:

  • connection between Pullmaflex plate and frame;
  • connection between adjustment cables and frame;
  • regulation casing seal.

Continue to Step 8

Connections incorrect

Connect affected parts correctly

Rivetting incorrect

Replace instable rivets

8

CHECK CUSHION AND SQUAB FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Completely remove seat upholstery Op. 7045A92 Front seat COVER, left or right - R.R. with seat removed

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • cushion module to sub-base
  • squab beneath cushion sub-base

Continue to Step 9

Bolts loosened

Restore the correct fastening

9

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the seat Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
