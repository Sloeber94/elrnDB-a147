INTRODUCTION

The operation of the clutch release differs according to the version.

The versions without Selespeed have a coaxial, hydraulically operated device known as the CSC system.

On versions fitted with a Selespeed gearbox the clutch release takes place through conventional linkage controlled by the electro-hydraulic unit [2127A hydraulic transmission external linkage] fitted on the gearbox casing which also controls the gear selection and engagement.
