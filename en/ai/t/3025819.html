Refitting

1. Open the glove compartment and disconnect the end of travel retainers.

2. Remove the bulb holder (2a) and switch (2b).

3. Disconnect the electrical connections (3a, 3b).

Removing

Check that the parts of the bulb holder / switch are not damaged and that the bulb is working properly.
