.

The VDC steering angle sensor is available from Spares together with the steering column switch unit and must not be dismantled from the latter under any circumstances. Therefore, for the reval of the steering column switch unit complete with steering angle sensor 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag .
