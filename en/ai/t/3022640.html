J20 REARSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, when the heated rear window button is pressed, neither the rear window nor the door mirrors warm up properly

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING FUSES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the specific heated rear window and rearview mirror supply fuses on the underfacia junction unit

Continue to Step 2

Blown fuses

Before changing the part, seek the cause of the problem

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) to the diagnostic socket and check:

  • that there are no errors in the climate control unit
  • that the heated rear window switch changes over to the parameters environment

Continue to Step 3

There are faults in the control unit.

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

When the heated rear window switch is operated, the displayed status (ON-OFF) does not change

Replace the climate control unit Op. 5040D15 control unit with automatic duel zone air conditioner knobs and buttons r r

3

CHECKING HEATED REAR WINDOW AND MIRROR POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Operating the heated rear window switch, check:

  • correct supply to the heated rear window and rear view mirrors See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Continue to Step 4

Power supply is correct

Continue to Step 4

Power supply not coming through

Continue to Step 5

4

CHECKING DEMISTING COIL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the continuity of the rear window and rear view mirror heating elements
  • earth connections See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Continue to Step 7

Rear window heating element broken

Replace the rearscreen Op. 7035B14 rear window glass r r includes cleaning of housing

Mirror heating element broken

Replace the mirror(s) Op. 7005R30 electrically controlled door mirror (one) - r+r

Earth connections not correct

Restore connections See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

5

CHECK ON HEATED REAR WINDOW

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • operation of heated rear window
  • heated rear window's specific earth (+ / -) and supply See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Continue to Step 7

Heated rear window fault

Replace heated rear window

Heated rear window supply failure

Restore connections See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Heated rear window earth failure

Continue to Step 6

6

CHECK ON UNDERFACIA JUNCTION UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • Continuity of internal connections of heated rear window control circuit. See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Continue to Step 7

Underfacia junction unit fault

Replace underfacia junction unit Op. 5505A10 electrical junction unit - r+r

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r
