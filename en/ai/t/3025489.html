3. Disconnect the door frame wiring.

4. Disconnect the door wiring retainers from the chassis.

5. Remove the wiring from the door frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Position the wiring in its housing in the door frame.

Connect the cable loom to the door frame using the retainers.

Connect the connector to the door frame (bayonet fitting).

Connect the electrical connection.

Check the operation of the electrical components connected.
