Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the display does not display the time correctly or at all.

Results of the tests

1

INSTRUMENT PANEL FUNCTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • That the display shows the correct time
  • That it is possible to adjust the time
  • That the clock moves forward
  • That the adjustment of the panel brightness has not been selected.
  • That the mode button has not been pressed
  • That no fault messages are displayed.

Continue to Step 2

The time displayed is not correct

Adjust the time

The time cannot be adjusted

Continue to Step 2

The clock is stuck

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

The time is not displayed because there are priority messages or adjustments in progress.

Remove the cause of the priority messages

The display/time cannot be seen properly

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Verify:

  • that there are no faults

Continue to Step 3

There are errors

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

3

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket.

In active diagnosis, check the condition of the display

Continue to Step 4

Display defective

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

4

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • connections between the left control panel and the instrument panel
  • connections between the left control panel and the earth
  • left control panel earth
  • See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 5

Connections defective; earth defective

Restore the correct connections; if necessary, replace the wiring: See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

5

LEFT CONTROL PANEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the left control panel is working properly: See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 6

Left control panel defective

Replace the left control panel Op. 5560B56 TRIM with SWITCHES/BUTTONS FOR MULTIFUNCTION DISPLAY AND HEADLAMP alignment ADJUSTMENT - R.R.

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
