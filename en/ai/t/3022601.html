CHECK ON CONTACT SURFACES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check type and surfaces of contact

Continue to Step 2

Front pads or discs vitrified

Replace the pads Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replaceor renew the disc surfaces

Rear pads or discs vitrified

Replace the pads Op. 3310B60 rear brake pads - replace - includes handbrake adjustment if necessaryor renew the disc surfaces

Dirty surfaces

Clean the surfaces in question

Front disc contact surfaces damaged

Grind the discs, or replace, if necessary Op. 3310A48 front brake disc - r r

Rear disc contact surfaces damaged

Grind the discs, or replace, if necessary Op. 3310B48 rear brake disc - r r

CHECK ON WHEEL CYLINDERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the wheel cylinders are not seized

Continue to Step 3

Front wheel cylinders seized

Replace front calipers Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

Front wheel cylinders seized

Replace rear calipers Op. 3310B36 rear brake calliper - r r

CHECK ON BRAKING ACTION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the braking effect does not decrease even after several braking actions

Continue to Step 4

The braking effect decreases significantly

Change the brake/clutch fluid Op. 0010T64 brake and or clutch fluid check level and top up as necessary
