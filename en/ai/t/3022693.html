N70 CONTROLS ON THE STEERING WHEEL NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The corresponding functions do not take place when the buttons on the front panel of the Connect are pressed. The corresponding function does not take place when the buttons on the steering wheel are operated.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check that there are no errors in the 'reading parameters' ambient at the Connect Node.

Continue to Step 2

Presence of faults

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in the 'Reading Parameters' ambient for the Connect Node, check there are no faults and that when the following buttons are pressed and released:

  • Volume + button
  • Volume - button
  • Right search button
  • Left search button
  • Input selection button

Continue to Step 4

The button(s) in question does not work (there are no changes in state)

Continue to Step 3

3

ELECTRICAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Continuity of the clock spring wiring.
  • Operation of the buttons on the steering wheel. See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 4

Clock spring wiring faulty.

Restore the correct operation of the clock spring wiring.

Clock spring wiring faulty.

Repair/replace the steering wheel

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the Connect module Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
