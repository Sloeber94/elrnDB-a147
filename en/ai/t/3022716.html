Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During starting, the 'Passenger Air Bag disabled' warning light does not come on.

The warning light should come on for 4 seconds indicating the performance of the control unit's initial test, and then go out.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) to the diagnostic socket and check:

  • that there are no faults in the AIR BAG control unit.

Continue to Step 2

There are faults in the control unit

Continue in accordance with the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • connection between air bag control unit and instrument panel See E7030 AIRBAG

Continue to Step 3

Wiring faulty

Change wiring See E7030 AIRBAG

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check it is working properly.

Fault persists

Replace air bag control unit Op. 5580C14 air bag ecu - r+r
