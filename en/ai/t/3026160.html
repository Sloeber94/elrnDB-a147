Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The Body Computer M1 receives the 'door open' input signals at pin 8 (left door) and pin 25 (right door) of connector E.

The Body Computer M1 is connected, via the CAN line, to the controls in the front door H44 according to the control logic described previously, which supplies the two door courtesy lights G51 and G52 .

The unit H44 is supplied by the line protected by fuse F39 of the underdashboard control unit B2 .

The 'low battery voltage' signal which switches off the courtesy lights if they are on, always arrives via the CAN E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES - Description .
