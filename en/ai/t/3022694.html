Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The 'voice command' function is managed by the telephone module which is incorporated in the Connect. The microphone is located on the outside.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Connect settings disable the voice command function.

Continue to Step 1

Settings not correct.

Restore the correct values.

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the following at the Connect Node.

In errors ambient

  • that there are no faults

Continue to Step 2

Presence of faults

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the Connect and check the continuity/insulation of the cable connecting the Connect and the microphone in the courtesy light. See E3570 INTEGRATED RADIO PHONE - Description

Continue to Step 3

Microphone wiring faulty

Renew the electrical connection.

Microphone faulty
