Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with the removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 1092G10 single engine belt r r .

Proceed with the removal 1092A10 Crankshaft services PULLEY - R.R. .

Proceed with the removal 1092G20 engine component single belt fixed tensioner - one - r + r .

1. Undo the lower bolts fixing the protective timing cover.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and the nut (1b) and remove the auxiliary units drive belt protection (1c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the remaining bolt (1a) and remove the protective timing cover (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and move the power assisted steering fluid reservoir (1b) without disconnecting the pipes.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the protective timing covers (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the protective timing covers back in their housings and secure them using the bolts.

Refit in its seating the power steering fluid reservoir and secure it with its bolts.

Proceed with the refitting 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

Place the protective timing cover back in its housing and secure it using the upper bolt.

Place the auxiliary units drive belt protection back in its housing and secure it using the bolts and the nut.

Tighten the lower bolts fixing the protective timing cover.

Proceed with the refitting 1092G20 engine component single belt fixed tensioner - one - r + r .

Proceed with the refitting 1092A10 Crankshaft services PULLEY - R.R. .

Proceed with the refitting 1092G10 single engine belt r r .

Proceed with the refitting 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Remove the car from the ramp.
