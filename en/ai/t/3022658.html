Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is turned on, the generator warning light does not come on.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check warning light operation while in active diagnosis mode

Continue to Step 2

The warning light does not come on

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check body computer power supply is correct, particularly the D+ and +15 signal: See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Continue to Step 3

Body computer power supply incorrect.

Restore correct power supply

3

Operation

Check the operation of the battery.

Battery not working

Replace the battery Op. 5530B10 battery - r+r
