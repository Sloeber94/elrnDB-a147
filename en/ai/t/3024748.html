Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Work with the power unit positioned on a platform and supported for safety purposes by a hydraulic hoist.

1. Undo the drain plug and drain the manual gearbox-differential oil.

Collect the gearbox and differential oil in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and nut (1b) and remove the reinforcement link (1c).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and nut (2b) and remove the power unit rear rigid mounting (2c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the intermediate drive shaft (1b).

2. Remove the dust cover.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Open the cover (1a), undo the nuts (1b) and (1c) and disconnect the electrical connections (1d) from the starter motor.
