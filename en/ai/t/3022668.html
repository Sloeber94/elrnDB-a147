Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the display readings are not clear and legible or they are missing completely.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

In active diagnosis, check the condition of the display

Continue to Step 2

The instrument panel is working except for the display where some or all segments are not working.

Replace the instrument panel Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

Instrument panel not working at all

Check the electrical equipment.

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • the supplies for the instrument panel, the connection between the instrument panel and the Body Computer : See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 3

Connections / supplies not correct

Restore the correct connections / supplies : See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

3

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
