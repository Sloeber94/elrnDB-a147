Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion, a noise is heard coming from the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

1

WIND NOISE LOCATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle until the speed is reached when the defect occurs in order to locate the area from which the wind noise is coming (if necessary, use bodywork adhesive tape)

Continue to Step 2

The wind noise is coming from the side doors

Verify:

  • door alignment Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment
  • door glass alignment [PR7005D15]
  • door housing surrounding seal [PR7005A34]
  • glass surrounding seal [PR7005D33]

The wind noise is coming from the hinged opening side window

Check glass alignment Op. 7035C52 (One), side WINDOW left or right - R.R.

The wind noise is coming from the rear pillar cover

Verify:

  • cover fixing Op. 7040H42 rear pillar trim - r r
  • cover alignment [PR7040H42]

The wind noise is coming from the wheel arch

Check wheel arch fixing/alignment Op. 7055B43 REAR WHEEL ARCH liner (one), left or right - R.R.

2

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
