Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Remove the circlips

2. Remove the pins (2a) and separate the connecting rods (2b) from the pistons (2c).

3. Remove the piston rings (3a) from the pistons using a suitable tool (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Check the alignment of the connecting rods using a suitable tool; if the connecting rod is not perfectly aligned, replace it.

Check that the difference in weight between the connecting rods, complete with half-bearings, caps and bolts, is within the recommended values.

The arrows in the diagram show the areas where material can be removed from in order to equalize the weight.

Assemble the connecting rods with the corresponding pistons so that the number stamped on the small end faces the large notches (for inlet valves) on the piston crown.

Fit the pin and secure it with its circlips.

Refit the piston rings using a suitable tool

1. Join the connecting rod to the piston so that:

for the left bank, arrow (1a) stamped on the piston is turned toward the same side as the stamp (1b) on the connecting rod;

for the right bank, arrow (1c) stamped on the piston is turned towards the opposite side to the stamp on the connecting rod.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
