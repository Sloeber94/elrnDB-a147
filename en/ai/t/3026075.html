5580CE Pretensioner check

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Disconnect the connector for the pretensioner and connect the special line closure resistor.

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

Cancel the errors and check that they have disappeared.

If the error persists, search for the fault in the wiring or in the control unit.

If the error does not occur again, the fault should be sought in the pretensioner.

Reconnect the pretensioner connector.
