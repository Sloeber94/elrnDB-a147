Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R. .

1. Undo bolt (1a) securing rear suspension longitudinal tie rod (1b) to the steering knuckle.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and withdraw bolt (2b) as far as necessary to disconnect front wishbone (2c) from the damper.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the nut of the bolt securing the rear suspension front wishbone to the rear suspension beam.

2. Undo the bolts securing the rear suspension crossmember to the body and lower as necessary.

Withdraw the screw of the bolt securing the rear suspension front wishbone to the beam.

3. Remove the rear suspension front wishbone.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the rear suspension front wishbone (1a) and secure to the beam (1b) by doing up the screw of the retaining bolt.

2. Tighten the bolts securing the rear suspension crossmember to the bodyshell to the recommended torque.

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R. .

1. Undo bolt (1a) securing rear suspension longitudinal tie rod (1b) to the steering knuckle.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and withdraw bolt (2b) as far as necessary to disconnect front wishbone (2c) from the damper.

3. Undo the nut of the bolt securing the rear suspension front wishbone to the rear suspension beam.

4. Remove the rear suspension front wishbone.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the rear suspension front wishbone (1a) and secure to the beam by doing up screw (1b) of the retaining bolt.

2. Connect front wishbone (2a) to the damper and secure using the bolt (2b) without tightening it.

3. Screw in bolt (3a) securing rear suspension longitudinal tie rod (3b) to the steering knuckle without tightening.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
