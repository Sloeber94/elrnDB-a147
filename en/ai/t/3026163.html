Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay T3 of the engine compartment control unit B1B from the direct line from the battery through fuse F3 located in junction unit B1A.

The coil of the relay is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed. The relay thereby supplies the high tone horn P10 and the low tone horn P11 .

The connection for the horn contact is via the clock spring connector D47 , which also transmits the signals for the driver's Air Bag and the steering wheel controls (for radio and telephone) E3510 RADIOE7030 AIRBAG .
