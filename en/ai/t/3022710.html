Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The motor cannot be heard running. (The interruption in the power supply causes the data memorzed in the control unit to be lost and the roof not to work).

Results of the tests

1

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • supply fuse
  • Supply and earth See E3060 SUN ROOF

Continue to Step 2

fuse damaged

Replace fuse

Connections not correct

Restore the connections. See E3060 SUN ROOF

2

RESETTING THE CONTROL UNIT MEMORY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Restore the sun roof control unit memory. Characteristic of working principle 7030B sliding roof

Continue to Step 4

Fault persists

Continue to Step 3

3

CHECKING OPERATION OF CONTROL SELECTOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • the operation of the control selector. See Test 7030BA CHECK ON CONDITION OF SUN ROOF SELECTOR
  • Connections between selector and sun roof control unit See E3060 SUN ROOF

Continue to Step 4

Selector defective

Replace selector Op. 7030B64 sun-roof control switch - r+r

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E3060 SUN ROOF

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace control unit/motor assembly Op. 7030B70 sun-roof control switch - r+r
