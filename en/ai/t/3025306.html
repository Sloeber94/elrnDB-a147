Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 4450B02 left front wheel r r .

1. Working from the left wheel arch, disconnect the return pipe from power steering box to the rigid pipe on the rigid pipe side.

Collect the power assisted steering fluid in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

1. Undo the connector (1a), disconnect the return pipe from the power assisted steering box to the rigid pipe (1b), power assisted steering box side, and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the return pipe from power steering box to the intermediate pipe (1a) back in its housing, connect it to the power steering box and secure by tightening the connector (1b) to the specified torque.

Bleed the power steering system as described below

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4-5 times.

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Remove the car from the ramps.

Start the engine and carry out 4 - 5 full steering wheel turn cycles (right and left).

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.
