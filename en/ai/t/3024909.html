1088D10 lower sleeve for coolant outlet from radiator - r + r

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Disconnect the lower coolant outlet hose from the radiator and drain the engine coolant.

Manually press the springs and remove the rapid attachment for the hose.

Recover the coolant which comes out in a suitable container.

2. Disconnect the lower radiator coolant outlet hose from the water pump rigid inlet pipe.

3. Remove the lower coolant outlet hose from the radiator.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the radiator lower coolant outlet hose back in its housing and connect it to the radiator and to the water pump rigid inlet pipe.

Refill the engine cooling system 0010T20 coolant change .

Remove the car from the ramp.

Place the radiator lower coolant outlet hose in its housing, connect it to the thermostat and tighten the band.
