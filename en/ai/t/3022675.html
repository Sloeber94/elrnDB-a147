L15 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT HEATING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan works properly and sends air into the interior compartment in accordance with set flow patterns, but this air does not ensure the required heating.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner) to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the climate control control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore connection between climate control system control unit and body computer See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner), carry out active diagnosis on the mixing motor (for manual versionj) or the two right and left mixing motors (for automatic version)

Continue to Step 3

Mixing motor connection faulty

Restore the connections See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

Fault in left mixing motor flap

Replace the motor Op. 5040D84 air conditioner left hand air mixer flap control motor - r r

Fault in right mixing motor flap

Replace the motor Op. 5040D84 air conditioner left hand air mixer flap control motor - r r

Fault in mixing ports

Replace the mixing motor ports see under Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

3

CHECKING HEATER PIPES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency and seal of the supply and return pipes to/from the heater.

Continue to Step 4

Pipes incorrectly connected

Restore the correct connection

Pipes damaged

Replace the pipes Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

4

CHECKING HEATER RADIATOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the heater radiator

Continue to Step 5

Radiator dirty or obstructed

Clean the radiator and replace if necessary Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
