1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF postition, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

Proceed with the removal 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r .

Draw off the fuel from the tank using suitable equipment.

Remove the handbrake boot.

1. Lift up the handbrake lever (1a) and loosen the nut (1b) tensioning the handbrake cables.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal 1076B22 rear exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

Proceed with the removal 1076B26 intermediate exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

1. Undo the fastenings and remove the exhaust pipe intermediate heat shield.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal 4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R. (Right side).

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the rear suspension crossmember.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and lower the rear suspension crossmember (2b), as necessary, using the hydraulic jack.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the fuel filler pipe from the tank.

2. Loosen the band and remove the fuel return pipe from the tank.

3. Disconnect the rapid attachments for the fuel vapour supply pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

4. Disconnect the rapid attachment for the fuel supply pipe from the multi-purpose valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Position a hydraulic jack, complete with suitable support, under the fuel tank.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and the nuts (2b) securing the fuel tank support belts.

3. Lower the hydraulic jack and remove the fuel tank.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the fuel tank back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

Tighten the nuts securing the fuel tank support belts.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the fuel tank.

Connect the rapid attachment for the fuel vapour supply pipe from the multipurpose valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Connect the rapid attachments for the fuel vapour supply pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

Connect the fuel return pipe to the tank and secure it to the band.

Connect thefuel filler pipe to the tank and secure it using the band.

Place the rear suspension crossmember back in its housing using the hydraulic jack and fix it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
