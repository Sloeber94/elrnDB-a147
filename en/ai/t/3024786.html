Refitting

Removing

1. Place the piston-connecting rod assemblies (1a) for the 2nd and 3rd cylinders, complete with half-bearings (1b), back in their housings using a suitable fitting tool (1c).

The piston-connecting rod assemblies should be fitted with the large references (for the inlet valves) on the piston crowns facing the inlet side.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

1. Fit the connecting rod caps (1a) for the 2nd and 3rd cylinders, complete with half-bearings (1b) and secure them by tightening the bolts (1c) to the recommended torque.

The big end bearing caps should be fitted so that the number stamped on them is facing the same side a the one stamped on the big end.
