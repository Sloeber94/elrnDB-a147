Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The milometer either does not work or does not work properly whilst driving.

The speedometer works properly. If the speedometer is faulty, see Table K03.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the instrument panel

Continue to Step 2

There are errors

Proceed in accordance with the instructions given by the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic socket, check:

  • the operation of the pointer through active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 3

Milometer not working

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

3

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check connections between instrument panel Body Computer and ABS control unit

Continue to Step 4

Faulty wiring

Restore/replace the wiring: See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
