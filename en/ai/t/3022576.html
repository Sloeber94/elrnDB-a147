Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when reverse gear is engaged, the reversing light does not come on

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Replace the bulb Op. 5550D24 reversing light - r+r

Bulb holder assembly faulty

Renew or replace the light cluster Op. 5550D24 reversing light - r+r

2

REVERSING LIGHT SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the reversing light switch is correctly fixed and working properly

Continue to Step 3

Reversing light switch faulty

Renew or replace the reversing light switch Op. 5550D22 reversing light switch - r+r

3

REVERSING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • reversing light fuse (services)
  • connections between DASHBOARD control unit and reversing lights switch; connections between DASHBOARD control unit and reversing light bulb; bulb earth : See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse (and search for the cause of its blowing)

Connections broken

Restore the connections: See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Bulb earth faulty

Restore the earth contact: See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

4

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
