Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the load carrying compartment moving cover.

2. Undo the bolts securing the anchoring rings.

3. Remove the mountings.

4. Undo the bolts securing the moulding.

5. Remove the moulding (5a), disengaging it from the inner retainers (5b) with the special tool.
