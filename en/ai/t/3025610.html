Before removing and refitting the control unit, carry out the following preliminary operations.

Place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it;

Disconnect the battery leads and insulate.

Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

Follow these precautions:

Do not strike the control unit and do not connect the electrical connection to the control unit before having fixed the control unit correctly. Make sure that the battery is disconnected.

If the control unit is being replaced, remove the removable part of the adhesive label from the new control unit and place it in a special file containing the vehicle details (model, registration date, chassis number) and the serial number of the old control unit. This file should be kept for future checks.
