Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is switched on, the symbols on the gearbox panel do not light up.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON LIGHTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • The condition of the background illumination LEDs

Continue to Step 2

Faulty LEDs

Replace LEDs

2

CHECK ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • Supply to gearbox panel
  • Fuse on dashboard unit
  • Correct connections between dashboard unit and gearbox panel
  • Correct connections between gearbox panel and earth
  • Earth

(see: See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX)

Continue to Step 3

Incorrect supply

Restore correct supply: See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Connections not correct

Restore correct connections: See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Fuse blown

Replace fuse (and search for the cause of its blowing)

Faulty earth

Restore the earth contact: See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX
