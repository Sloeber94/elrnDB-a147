1008C16 body link bracket - r r

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Proceed with the removal 1008C10 power unit upper reaction link r r .

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the upper bracket on the bodyshell for the reaction rod (1b).

The above mentioned nuts also secure the right front shock absorber to the bodyshell.

2. Undo the nut (2a) fixing the mounting bracket (2b) for the wheel rpm sensor connection and the fuel pipe.

3. Undo the nut (3a) and the bolt (3b) and remove the lower bracket on the bodyshell for the reaction rod (3c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the lower bracket on the bodyshell for the reaction rod (1a) back in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolt (1b) and the nut (1c) to the recommended torque.
