1820B40 FLEXIBLE PIPE from clutch reservoir to (pump) - master cylinder R.R.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Drain the brake-clutch fluid reservoir using a suitable syringe.

1. Loosen the bands, disconnect and remove the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder back in its housing and tighten the bands.

Bleed the air and top up the level in the hydraulic clutch system 0010T67 hydraulic clutch system bleed air .

Bleed the air from the hydraulic braking system 0010T65 hydraulic brake system bleed air .

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Remove the protective cap on the master cylinder bleed valve.

Connect the collection system (pipe and reservoir) to the bleed valve on the clutch operating cylinder.

Operate the clutch pedal until the brake-clutch fluid reservoir is empty.

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder, reservoir side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Loosen the band, disconnect the hose from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder on the master cylinder side and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder back in its housing and tighten the bands.

Bleed the air and top up the level in the hydraulic clutch system 0010T67 hydraulic clutch system bleed air .

Bleed the air from the hydraulic braking system 0010T65 hydraulic brake system bleed air .

Proceed with refitting 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with refitting 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

Proceed with refitting 7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R. .
