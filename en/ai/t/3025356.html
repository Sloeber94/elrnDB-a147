Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If the front wheel toe-in is not correct, adjust it as described below.

Straighten the steering wheel perfectly, and lock it in this position with a suitable tool.

Loosen the steering box gaiter clips on the steering tie-rods.

1. Slacken the steering tie-rod nuts.

2. Screw or unscrew the steering tie-rods until the specified toe-in is obtaIned, keeping the front wheels aligned.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tighten the tie-rod nuts to the specified torque.
