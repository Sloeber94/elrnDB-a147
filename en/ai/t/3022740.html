Applies to: all engines; all production periods

While driving with the roof fully open, noise can be heard from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise can heard only when the sun-roof is open

Continue to Step 1

The noise occurs with the roof closed

See table S76 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED

The noise occurs with the roof tilted open

See table S77 SUN ROOF NOISY / WTH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • Roof panel to runners;
  • Complete roof mechanism to the bodyshell

Continue to Step 2

Panel fastening faulty

Re-attach correctly Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r

Complete mechanism to bodyshell

Restore the correct fastening Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Checking operation of sun-roof devices:

  • With the roof tilted open, ensure the rubber pads on the rear parts of the slides are present
  • With the roof tilted open, check the end stop springs and central channel slides are undamaged and properly fastened
  • With the roof fully open, check the spoiler rubber end stop pads are undamaged and properly fastened

Continue to Step 3

Sun roof fastenings and/or operating devices faulty

Renew all the faulty components and the fastening of the entire mechanism Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
