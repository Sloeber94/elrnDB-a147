7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR

1. Undo the bolt fixing the radiator/condenser upper support.

2. Move the radiator/condenser assembly slightly backwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the air duct.

2. Remove the air duct downwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts for the pipe connectors.

2. Disconnect the connectors.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the condenser to the radiator.

4. Remove the condenser downwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the condenser is intact and is not damaged and does not have deposits of dirt on the finned surface.

Position the condenser.

Tighten the bolts fixing the condenser to the radiator.

Connect the pipe connectors.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors.

Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Tighten the bolt to the recommended torque.

7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR

1. Undo the bolt fixing the radiator/condenser upper support.

2. Move the radiator/condenser assembly slightly backwards.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Working in front of the right front wheel, undo the bolt fixing the air duct.

2. Remove the air duct.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the power assisted steering fluid pipe (1b) to the side.

2. Undo the bolts and disconnect the connectors for the climate control system pipes from the condenser.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Release the retaining buttons for the air duct using the special tool.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the air duct to the radiator.

3. Move the profiles for the air duct (3a) slightly and undo the bolts (3b) fixing the condenser.

4. Remove the condenser (4a) and the air duct (4b) together.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the condenser is intact and is not damaged and does not have deposits of dirt on the finned surface.

Position the condenser and the air duct together.

Tighten the bolts fixing the condenser.

Undo the bolts fixing the air duct to the radiator.

Engage the air duct retaining buttons.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A refrigerant. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Connect the connectors for the climate control system pipes and tighten the bolts.
