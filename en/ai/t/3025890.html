Removing

Refitting

1. Lower the left-hand squab

2. Remove the armrest embellishment.

3. Remove the armrest (3a) disengaging it from the retainers (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Check that the armrest is not damaged.

Position the armrest and engage the retainers.

Fit the panel and raise the squab.
