7040B22 luggage compartment/load platform moulding - r+r

1. Unscrew the bolts

2. Remove the lock striker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lock striker is not damaged.

1. Position the lock striker.

2. Screw in the retaining bolts without tightening.

3. Position the striker in the correct position, using the slotted holes for the bolts. Permitted adjustment movements are indicated by arrows.

4. Tighten the bolts.
