Removing

Refitting

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF postition, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Connector

Battery

Connector

1

Accelerator pedal potentiometer

K55

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit the accelerator pedal with integrated potentiometer in its seating and secure it with its bolts.

Connect the electrical connection to the accelerator pedal potentiometer.

Connect the battery terminal (-).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Guida a destra
