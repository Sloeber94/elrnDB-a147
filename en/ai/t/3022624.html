Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the rear fog lamp switch is operated with the dipped beam headlamps on (or front fog lamps on), the rear fog lamp does not work, and this fault is indicated by the relevant warning light.

If the rear fog lamp does not work and the check on the Body Computer does not indicate this I30 LIGHTS CHECK NOT WORKING.

If the dipped beam headlamps do not work either, first see I05 DIPPED HEADLAMPS NOT WORKING
