Removing

Refitting

1. Slightly heat the lettering to be removed.

2. Detach the lettering using a plastic spatula.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lettering is undamaged.

Use heptane to clean the area of the bodywork affected by refitting.

1. Apply adhesive tape (1a) to the outer edge of the lettering, to hold the parts in place, before remvoing the protection (1b) from the adhesive side of the letters.

This is necessary to keep the letters in the correct position in relation to one another because they are separate from one another.

2. Remove protection (1b) from the adhesive side of the letters and position the lettering, taking care to align them horizontally. Then bond the adhesive.

3. Remove the adhesive tape.
