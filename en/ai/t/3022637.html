J04 BACK WINDOW WIPER NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the rearscreen wiper does not work properly, but the rearscreeen washer works

Results of the tests

1

REARSCREEN WIPER MOTOR SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • motor connection to earth
  • connection between starter motor and junction unit See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 2

Connection broken

Restore the connections: See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

2

REARSCREEN WIPER MOTOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the rearscreen wiper motor is working properly

Continue to Step 3

Motor faulty

Replace the rearscreen wiper motor Op. 5050C42 rear wiper motor - r+r

3

CONTROL UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the electronic control device is working properly and, in particular, that the pins are not oxidized or distorted

Continue to Step 4

Control unit faulty

Replace the windscreen wiper control unit Op. 5050B38 windscreen wiper assembly - r+r or repair

4

CHECKING STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly: in particular, the rearscreen wiper switch: Characteristic of working principle 5050 SCREEN WASHERS AND HEADLAMP WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 5

Steering column switch unit not working properly

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists
