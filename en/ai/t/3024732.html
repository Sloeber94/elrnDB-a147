0010T71 power steering fluid check level and top up if necessary

Path

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

1. Check that the power steering fluid level corresponds to the MAX reference on the reservoir or corresponds to the upper notch (maximum level) on the dipstick in the reservoir cap.

Power steering fluid level should be checked with the vehicle on a flat surface and the engine switched off and cold.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

If the oil level is lower than MAX, top up with the specified fluid.

HYDRAULIC POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM

TUTELA GI/A

1.2

The table shows the total quantity of fluid in the power steering circuit.

Start the engine and wait for the level in the reservoir to stabilise.

With the engine running, turn the steering wheel fully to the right and lleft several times.

Top up until the fluid level is at MAX, then refit the cap.

Prevent the power steering fluid coming into contact with hot parts of the engine: it is inflammable.

Fluid consumption is very low; if another top-up is required after a short time, check the system for leaks.
