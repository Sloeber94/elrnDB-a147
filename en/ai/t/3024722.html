0010T21 coolant check density and top up if necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the engine is very hot, do not remove the coolant reservoir cap due to the danger of burns.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

1. Ensure the coolant level in the reservoir is between the MIN and MAX marks visible on the reservoir.

Check coolant level with the engine cold and the car level.

2. If the level is insufficient, unscrew the coolant reservoir cap and top up with recommended coolant to the MAX mark.

The antifreeze in the cooling circuit guarantees protection down to a temperature of -40° C.
