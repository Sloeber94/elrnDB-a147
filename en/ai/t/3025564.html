Removing

Refitting

1. Rotate the cover in an anti-clockwise direction and remove it.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections.

3. Release the retaining spring moving it sideways.

4. Extract the bulb.

If the lamp is working properly, avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
