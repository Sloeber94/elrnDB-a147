Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat will not go up or down when the lever is operated

Test the mechanism with someone sitting on the seat.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING GEAR OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the outer seat guard Op. 7045A43 seat exterior guard - r r. Check that there are no obstacles or foreign material

Continue to Step 2

Presence of obstacles or dirt along the device mechanism

Clean the device and restore operation

2

CHECKING SEAT RETAINING SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition of the lever retaining system and the seat lift ratchet

Continue to Step 3

Lever or retaining elements broken

Replace the lever or other affected parts Op. 7045A43 seat exterior guard - r r

Parts incorrectly fastened

Restore the correct fastening

3

CHECKING SEAT LIFT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the outer seat guard Op. 7045A43 seat exterior guard - r r. Check the seat lifting device In particular, check the condition of the device gear teeth and ensure it is properly secured.

Continue to Step 4

Device faulty

Repair or replace the device Op. 7045A36 drivers seat manual height adjustment device - r r.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
