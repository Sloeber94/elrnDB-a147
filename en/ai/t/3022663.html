Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The engine management control unit warning light does not come on. The CODE warning light comes on and goes out as normal on versions with low line instruments, whilst it does not come on on versions with high line instruments.

The CODE light going out normally on low line versions and not coming on on high line versions means that the dialogue between the two control units has had a positive outcome. The failure of the engine management control unit warning light to come on is due to a fault in the warning light operating circuit. The failure to come on is caused by supply system problems.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and check:

  • that there is a dialogue between the control unit and the diagnostic equipment
  • that there are no errors in the control unit

Continue to Step 2

Dialogue not activated

Restore the connections between the control unit and the diagnostic equipment See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Errors present in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment
