Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Functional description

The Body Computer M1 is supplied directly by the battery from the line protected by the maxifuse CPL1 of B5 at pin 18 of connector F of the interface with the underdashboard control unit B2.

The Body Computer M1 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 9 of connector F of the interface with the underdashboard control unit B2 : this signal is designed to 'wake up the network'.

Pin 10 of connector C and pin 8 of connector D M1 are earthed.

The nodes connected to the Body Computer M1 via the C-CAN are:

  • engine management control unit M10 ;
  • ABS control unit M50 (which is not connected directly, but via M10 );
  • steering sensor unit for VDC K58 ;
  • 'SELESPEED' gearbox control unit M54 - if fitted - (which is not connected directly, but via K58)

The following nodes are connected to the Body Computer M1 via B-CAN:

  • climate control system control unit M70 ;
  • instrument panel E50 ;
  • radio or radio-phone-navigator P20 ;
  • driver's door control unit H44 .
