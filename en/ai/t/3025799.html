7040A26 steering column trim - r+r

Removing

Refitting

7040A37 fuse box cover - r r

1. If necessary, regulate the steering wheel height adjustment lever.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the lower trim.

3. Remove the lower trim, releasing it from the inner retainers and the steering wheel.

4. Undo the bolts fixing the upper trim.

5. Remove the top trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trims are not damaged.

Position the upper trim and secure it using the bolts.

Position the lower trim, engaging the retainers.

Tighten the screws.

7040A37 fuse box cover - r r

Return the steering wheel to the correct position.
