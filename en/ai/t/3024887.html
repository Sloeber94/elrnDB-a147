Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF postition, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

Proceed with the removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

Drain the air conditioner refrigerant system 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling .

1. Disconnect the rapid attachment for the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter, active charcoal filter side.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts and move the power steering box as far forward as necessary.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Release the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter from the fastenings on the engine compartment bulkhead.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the protections (1b) for the fuel supply pipe and the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r .

Remove the handbrake boot.

Proceed with the removal 1076B22 rear exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

Proceed with the removal 1076B26 intermediate exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

Proceed with the removal 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel .

Tighten the rear suspension beam bolts provisionally.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the crossmember.

1. Undo bolt (1a) and disconnect flange (1b) of the pipe from condenser to intermediate connector, intermediate connector side.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

2. Release the air conditioning system hoses from the fastenings on the side panel.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the supply and return pipes from the evaporator (1b), evaporator side.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

1. Release the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter from the underbody fastenings and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter back in its housing and secure it to the underbody using the fastenings.

1. Connect the supply and return pipes from the evaporator (1a), evaporator side and secure them tightening the bolt (1b) to the recommended torque.

Replace the O-Ring seals for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

Lubricate the threads of the connectors using anti-freeze.
