Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON and the side lights on, the instrument lights up, but the brightness remains constant when the controls are adjusted.

the side lights are working properly

Results of the tests

1

CONTROLS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the + / - mode buttons on the Computer left controls panel are working properly: See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

Continue to Step 2

Switches faulty

Replace the left control panel Op. 5560B56 TRIM with SWITCHES/BUTTONS FOR MULTIFUNCTION DISPLAY AND HEADLAMP alignment ADJUSTMENT - R.R.

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • Connections between left control panel and NQS;
  • Connections between controls panel and junction unit

Continue to Step 3

Connections / supplies not correct

Restore correct connections / supplies: See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

3

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check the operation of the panel light through the active diagnosis of the instrument panel.

Continue to Step 4

Light does not change, instrument panel defective

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
