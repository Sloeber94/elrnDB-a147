Removing

Refitting

Lift the bonnet lid.

1. Release and lift up the nut cover.

2. Undo the nut.

3. Remove the washer.

4. Remove the front windscreen wiper.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the front windscreen wiper is not damaged.

Refit the front windscreen wiper in the correct position and secure using the nut and the washer.
