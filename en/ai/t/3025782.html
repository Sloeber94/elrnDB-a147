7035A04 WINDSCREEN - Removing penetration

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After ascertaining the lcoation of the leak, remove one of the internal components covering the edge of the windscreen.

1. Wet with soapy water the external area where the leak occurs.

2. Blow compressed air from inside and identify the point of the leak.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If the leak is small, seal the relevant part from the inside and wait for the sealant to dry. Then repeat the test.

If the leak is large, 7035A14 (Bonded type) windscreen GLASS - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of glass and housing in bodyshell .

Refit the removed parts.
