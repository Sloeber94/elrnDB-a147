Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the fuel reserve warning light does not come on when the tank is empty.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) to the diagnostic socket and check:

Verify:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer;
  • that there are no faults on the instrument panel

Continue to Step 2

Faults in control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) connected to the diagnostic socket, check via active diagnosis that the warning light is still on.

Continue to Step 3

The warning light does not come on

Panel defective, replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

3

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
