3340B62 rear connection central pipe - r r

Path
Procedures > 33 BRAKING SYSTEM > 3340 A(B.S. ANTI-LOCK BRAKING SYSTEM .) > 3340B abs system pipes > 3340B62 rear connection central pipe - r r
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-a147/en/#/3025261

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 3340A12 A'B.S. system. - electronic and hydraulic CONTROL UNIT R.R. .

Proceed with removal 3340A13 abs central mount - r r .

Proceed with removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Left side).

Proceed with removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Release the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the rear brake pipe connector from the fastenings on the engine bulkhead.

1. Remove the brake pipe protection.

2. Undo the connector (2a) and remove the rigid pipe from the hydraulic unit to the connector on the pipes to the rear brakes (2b), releasing it from its attachments.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit in its seating the rigid pipe from the hydraulic unit to the connector on the pipes to the rear brakes, and secure it to the underbody.

Tighten the connector on the rigid pipe from the hydraulic unit to the connector on the pipes to the rear brakes, connector side, to the recommended torque.
