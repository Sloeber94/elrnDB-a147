Proceed with the removal 1032B10 toothed timing belt - r + r for timing adjustment or replacement .

Proceed with the removal1032B52 toothed driven pulley (one) - r + r (intake side and exhaust side)

1. Undo bolts (1a) and move cam angle sensor (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the camshaft caps (2b).

3. Remove camshafts (3a) and front oil seals (3b)

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Refitting

Removing

Place the camshafts back in their housings.

Fit the camshaft caps and fix them tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Do not fit the 2nd intake side camshaft cap and the 3rd exhaust side camshaft cap because the engine timing adjustment templates will be fitted in their place.
