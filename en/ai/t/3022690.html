N35 I.C.S. TELEPHONE DEVICE NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This table does not deal with faults caused by a malfunction of the service provider or the telephone function.

the telephone is incorporated in the Connect node.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the service provider card (SIM Card) is working properly

Continue to Step 1

SIM card not present or not correctly positioned, unsuitable.

Restore correct operation

1

VOICE COMMAND CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the voice recognition is working properly.

Continue to Step 2

Microphone faulty.

See table N71 CONNECT DOES NOT RECEIVE VOICE MESSAGES

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the plausibility of the following parameters at the Connector Node in the parameters reading ambient:

  • Telephone
  • Telephone menu button (TEL)
  • Phone card

Continue to Step 3

Telephone signal not present.

Replace the Connect

Signal that TEL button is always pressed/released.

See table N28 THE FUNCTIONS ARE NOT SELECTED WHEN THE BUTTONS ARE PRESSED

Signal that phone card is missing.

Replace the Connect module Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the Connect and check that the telephone aerial connector is correctly inserted in its housing

Continue to Step 4

Connector disconnected from the Connect.

Insert the connector in its housing See E3570 INTEGRATED RADIO PHONE - Description

4

AERIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the coaxial aerial cable and the actual aerial - check aerial amplifier supply See E3570 INTEGRATED RADIO PHONE - Description

Continue to Step 5

Aerial cable faulty

Renew/replace the aerial lead

Amplifier supply leads faulty

Renew/replace the supply lead

Amplifier and/or aerial faulty

Replace the amplifier and/or aerial

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the aerial support.
