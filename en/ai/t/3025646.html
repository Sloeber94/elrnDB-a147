7005D24 front door window rear guide - r r

1. Unscrew the bolt.

2. Undo the nut.

3. Working via the opening (3a) disconnect the rod (3b) connected to the lock from the handle side,

4. Disconnect the rod connected to the handle from the lock side.

5. Move the handle and rod forwards and extract them.

6. If necessary, disconnect and remove the rod connected to the handle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the handle is undamaged.

If removed, connect the rod connected to the handle.

Fit the handle with linkage.

Connect the rod connected to the handle on the lock side.

Connect the rod connected to the lock on the handle side.

Tighten the nut.

Tighten the bolt.

Check that the outer handle and lock are working properly.
