Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when reverse gear is engaged, the reversing light does not come on

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs Op. 5550D24 reversing light - r+r

Bulb holder assembly faulty

Renew or replace the light unit Op. 5550D24 reversing light - r+r

2

CHECKING REVERSING LIGHT SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the reversing light switch is correctly fixed and working properly

Continue to Step 3

Reversing light switch faulty

Renew or replace the reversing light switch Op. 5550D22 reversing light switch - r+r

3

CHECKING REVERSING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • reversing light fuse (services)
  • connections between junction unit and reversing light switch
  • connections between FACIA control unit and reversing light bulb
  • bulb earth See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse (and search for the cause of its blowing)

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Bulb earth faulty

Restore the earth contact See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
