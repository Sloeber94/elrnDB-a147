Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

Fans

The two-speed electric cooling fans increase the heat dissipation capacity of the radiator and/or air conditioning condenser. It is controlled directly by the injection control unit in accordance with a specific operating logic 1056 PETROL INJECTION SYSTEM.

Combined sender unit for coolant temperature gauge and overheating warning light

Mounted on the cylinder head, gearbox side, it indicates the coolant temperature and controls the warning light:

  • contacts open 112 ± 3° C
  • contacts closed 122 ± 2° C

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TS; all production periods
