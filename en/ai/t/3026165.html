Functional description

The electronic device inside the steering column switch unit H5 and the controls on the right lever are supplied at pin 11 of connector A by the line 'controlled by the ignition' (INT/A) of the relay T12 protected by the fuse F43, both housed in the underdashboard control unit B2 .

The electronic section of the steering column switch unit H5 which manages the rearscreen wiper function is supplied at pin 2 of connector C from the same line 'controlled by the ignition' (INT/A) for the relay T12 protected by the fuse F52, housed in the underdashboard control unit B2 .

When the rearscreen wiper function is switched on, the steering column switch unit H5 sends the control signal to the rearscreen wiper motor N16 from pin 3 of connector C; pin 4, on the other hand, receives the earth signal coming from the motor end of travel cam which stops the operation. The motor N16 is then earthed.

The windscreen/rearscreen washer switch, when operated, sends two signals from pins 10 and 12 of connector A which operate the windscreen/rearscreen washer pump N22 directly.

Pin 3 of connector A receives the automatic rearscreen wiper activation signal coming from the Body Computer M1 , pin 1 of connector A E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS .
