With the ignition on, when the stalk unit lever is set to automatic, the windscreen wiper does not work when there is water on the windscreen

Results of the tests

1

WIRING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check connections between:

  • battery;
  • junction unit under dashboard;
  • stalk unit supply;
  • rain sensor
  • windscreen wiper motor: See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 2

Wiring broken

Restore the wiring: See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Fuse blown

Replace fuse and search for the cause of the problem

Faulty earth

Repair the earth
