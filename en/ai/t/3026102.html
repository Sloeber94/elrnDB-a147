Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

In addition to the ABS EBD system, some 147 versions may be fitted with a drive control system (ASR/MSR) or a vehicle stability control system (VDC).

These two systems are a further development of the ABS 5.7 system featuring improved specifications.

By means of its yaw and steering wheel angular position sensors, the VDC system is able to monitor whole car dynamics and increase active safety.
