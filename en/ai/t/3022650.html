Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the outside temperature is not displayed by the milometer.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • That the adjustment of the panel brightness has not been selected.
  • That the mode button has not been pressed
  • That there are no dashes displayed in place of the temperature
  • That there are no failure messages displayed

Continue to Step 1

The display shows other information

Restore the display conditions using the left control panel

There are dashes in place of the temperature.

Continue to Step 2

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect an Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Verify:

  • that there are no faults
  • the temperature measured by the driver's door Connector

Continue to Step 2

There are errors

Proceed in accordance with the instructions given by the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • That the connections between the outside temperature sensor and the driver's door connector are correct

Continue to Step 3

Connections defective

Restore the correct connections; if necessary, replace the wiring: See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL
