Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat belt tab does not attach to the buckle.

For the rear belts, the tab for the centre place can only be attached to the corresponding centre buckle. Therefore if the two components do not correspond they cannot be attached

0

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no foreign bodies preventing attachment between the tab and the buckle

Continue to Step 1

Remove the impediments preventing the tab and buckle from being attached
