Follow the safety instructions 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS .

Drain the coolant 0010T84 coolant for air conditioning system dehydrate and recharge .

Position a suitable container for recovering the coolant fluid.

Raise the vehicle.

1. Working underneath the engine compartment, loosen the bands retaining the coolant supply and return pipes to the air conditioning.

2. Disconnect the pipes from the connectors and let the coolant drain into the container.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Guida a destra

Lower the vehicle.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the pipes to the air conditioning evaporator.

2. Disconnect the pipes from the connector to the evaporator.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Guida a destra
