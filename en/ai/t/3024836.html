Refitting

Removing

Place the single fuel manifold pipe, complete with injectors, back in its housing. Take care to fit the injectors in their housings by pressing with both hands.

Tighten the two bolts fixing the single fuel manifold pipe.

Secure the front fuel delivery pipe to the fastening on the air chamber.

Connect the rapid attachment for the front fuel supply pipe, fuel manifold pipe side.

Connect the electrical connections to the injectors.

Proceed with the refitting 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

Connect the battery terminal (-).
