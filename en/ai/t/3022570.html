Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

The engagement of the gears is difficult; there is also noise in the gearbox area (the gearbox grates)

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the clutch releases properly: with the engine running, press the clutch pedal and engage a gear

Continue to Step 3

The gearbox always grates in any gear: the clutch does not disengage

See table E01 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY

2

CHECK ON ELECTROHYDRAULIC UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

2.0 TS

Cambio robotizzato

Using the diagnostic equipment, carry out the operations described See table C01 GEAR CONTROL HARDENINGSteps 4 - 5 - 6

Continue to Step 3

No problems are found in the electrohydraulic unit, the geabox noise persists.

Check the clutch assembly See table E01 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLYSteps 4 - 6 - 6

3

CHECK GEAR SELECTION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pre-synchronizers

Continue to Step 4

Pre-synchronizers damaged

Replace the pre-synchronizers

4

CHECK ON SYNCHRONIZER RINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox in accordance with the procedures; in particular check that the synchronizer rings are not worn on the internal surfaces

Continue to Step 5

Seals worn

Replace the synchronizers

5

CHECK GEARS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox in accordance with the procedures, checking in particular the gear front teeth; they should not be excessively worn or chipped

Continue to Step 6

Gears worn

Replace the faulty gears

6

CHECK HUBS AND SLIDING SLEEVES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox in accordance with the procedures and check that the hubs and sliding sleeves do not show wear or excessive play.

Continue to Step 7

Sleeves or hubs excessively worn

Replace worn parts
