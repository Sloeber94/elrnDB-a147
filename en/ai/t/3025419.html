Removing

Refitting

Drain the coolant (5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling ).

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR

1. Undo the bolts fixing the pipe to the condenser and to the drier filter.

2. Disconnect the connectors for the pipe from the condenser and from the drier filter.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

3. Recover the pipe.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Reposition the pipe.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors.

Use only green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

Lubricate the threads of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Connect the pipe connectors and tighten the bolts.
