S12 ENGINE BUZZING'

Path
Fault diagnosis > S NOISY' / VIBRATIONS > S12 ENGINE BUZZING'
Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

There is a constant, loud noise coming from the area of the engine, which is repeated at constant intervals

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING AUXILIARY DRIVE BELT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the whistling disappears when the belt is disconnected.

Do not let the engine run for long with the belt disconnected

Continue to Step 2

The whistling disappears:

See table S10 ENGINE WHILSTLING / SERVICES BELT NOISY'

The whistling remains constant

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECKING ALTERNATOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the alternator and, in particular

  • condition of bearing
  • that the pulley is correctly fixed

Continue to Step 3

Alternator bearing damaged

Overhaul the alternator Op. 5530A18 alternator - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check including component replacement if necessary

Pulley fixing not correct

Restore the correct pulley fastening Op. 5530A30 alternator pulley or fan - r+r with alternator removed

3

CHECK TIMING BELT TENSION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the belt is not too taut

Continue to Step 4

Belt tension excessive

Restore correct tension

4

CHECK TIMING BELT TENSIONER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the belt tensioners and, in particular, the condition of the bearings

Continue to Step 5

Fixed belt tensioner bearing damaged

Replace the fixed belt tensioner Op. 1092G20 engine component single belt fixed tensioner - one - r + r

Mobile belt tensioner bearing damaged

Change the mobile belt tensioner Op. 1092G14 engine component single belt mobile tensioner - r + r

5

WATER PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the water pump and, in particular the condition of the bearing

Continue to Step 6

Water pump bearing damaged

Replace the water pump Op. 1088C10 water pump - r + r

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
