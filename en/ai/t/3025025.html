1048B48

Raise the vehicle.

1. Disconnect the air inlet sleeve from the inlet manifold.

2. Disconnect the air inlet sleeve from the heat exchanger.

3. Undo the bolts securing the heat exchanger to the mounting bracket.

4. Prise pins (4a) of the air-air heat exchanger (4b) out of the lower rubber retaining bushes and remove.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the turbocharger air-air heat exchanger and secure with bolts.

Connect the air delivery sleeves to the inlet manifold and the air inlet sleeve from the heat exchanger.

Lower the vehicle.

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with removal 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with removal 1048B48 .

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side.

2. Loosen the band and disconnect the air supply duct from heat exchanger to inlet manifold, heat exchanger side.

3. Undo the supercharging air/air heat exchanger fixing bolts.

4. Remove the supercharging air/air heat exchanger after having released the pins from the lower fastening bushes.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the supercharging air/air heat exchanger in its housing fastening the pins to the lower bushes.

Tighten the bolts fixing the supercharging air/air heat exchanger.

Cconnect the air supply hose from heat exchanger to inlet manifold, heat exchanger side, and tighten the band.

Connect the supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side, and secure with a band.

Proceed with refitting 1048B48 .

Proceed with refitting 7210E10 R.R. CENTRE PILLAR .

Proceed with refitting 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. .

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Remove the car from the ramps.
