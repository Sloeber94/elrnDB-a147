Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The current socket/cigar lighter P50 is supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F44, both contained in the unit under the dashboard B2 .

The lighting up of the cigar lighter P50 with side lights on is controlled by the Body Computer M1 - pin 18 of connector C.
