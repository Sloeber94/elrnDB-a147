When some of the buttons on the front panel are pressed the corresponding functions do not take place

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check that for errors at the Connect Node in the 'Reading Parameters' ambient

Continue to Step 2

Presence of faults

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in the 'Reading Parameters' ambient for the Connect Node, check there are no faults and there is a change of state (pressed/released) in the following parameters:

  • Input selection button
  • Mute button
  • Menu button

Continue to Step 3

The button(s) in question does not work (there are no changes in state).

Replace the Connect module Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the Connect Op. 5580P20 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
