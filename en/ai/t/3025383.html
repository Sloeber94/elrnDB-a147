Removing

Check that the duplex pipe is not damaged and that there are no cracks which would affect the seal.

Reposition the duplex pipe and connect it to the connects and to the retaining band.

The heater water supply and return pipes have white or green coloured ends to prevent them from being incorrectly fitted after operations where they are both removed.

Tighten the duplex pipe retaining bands.

Position the pipe from the filter to the throttle casing and secure it using the bands.

Secure the wiring using the band.

Connect the electrical connection.
