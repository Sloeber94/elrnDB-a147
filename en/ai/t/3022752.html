Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When it is raining or after washing, water leaks in through the sunroof

Results of the tests

1

CHECK SUNROOF DRAINS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check sunroof drain pipes

In particular, ascertain:

  • Condition of pipes;
  • No dirt, leaves, etc. are present

Continue to Step 2

Dirt in drainage channels

Clean the drainage channels If necessary refit correctly

2

CHECK SUNROOF SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check sun-roof seal In particular, ascertain:

  • No wrinkles in the seal;
  • Condition of the seal;

Continue to Step 3

Fitting of seal incorrect

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7030B22 glass seal - r+r with glass removed

3

CHECK SUNROOF ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check sun-roof installation. In particular, ascertain:

  • Correct sunroof - frame alignment;
  • Correct adherence of seal to roof

Continue to Step 4

Faulty fitting of the sunroof

Refit the sunroof correctly Op. 7030B54 sun roof systems

4

CHECK THAT BOLTS SECURING SUNROOF RUNNERS ARE SECURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the bolts fixing the runners to the sunroof frame are secure

Continue to Step 5

Bolts fitted incorrectly

Restore the correct fixing of the runners Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

5

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
