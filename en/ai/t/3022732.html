Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle driving, there is a constant noise from the front, especially when steering

Results of the tests

1

ROAD TEST

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test at low speed steering to the left and to the right

Continue to Step 2

There is a 'grinding' noise which changes drastically when the steering angle changes

Replace the constant velocity joint on the side that the noise is coming from

A 'turning' noise is heard

Overhaul the differential Op. 2110D10 differential inner case - r + r with manual gearbox removed - includes gearbox dismantle and rebuild

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
