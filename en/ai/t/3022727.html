S22 NOISE' GEARBOX / IN NEUTRAL

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods

With the vehicle stationary, the gear lever in neutral and the engine running, there is a high frequency, low intensity noise in the area of the gearbox. The fault is often accompanied by an oil leak from the gearbox: also see Table X15

Results of the tests

0

FIRST CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Press the clutch pedal and check whether the noise disappears or decreases

Continue to Step 1

Noise remains constant

Continue to Step 3

1

GEARBOX/DIFFERENTIAL FLUID LEVEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox/differential fluid level Op. 2110A04 Manual gearbox and differential OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary

Continue to Step 2

Level too low

Restore the correct level and road test the vehicle.

Continue to Step 3

2

GEARBOX BEARING CONDITIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings. In particular, check the gearbox bearings: they should not show signs of wear

Continue to Step 3

Bearings worn

Replace the bearings Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

3

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • thrust bearing;
  • clutch plate fouling (oil or grease);
  • that the clutch plate is not distorted;
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring;
  • that there is no damage to the flexible coupling springs;

Continue to Step 4

Clutch disc or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Thrust bearing faulty

Replace the bearing Op. 1820A30 thrust bearing - r + r with gearbox removed

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
