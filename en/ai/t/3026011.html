1016E38 single cylinder head or (one) l or r, removed - grinding

Path

Kind

Applies to
engines: 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Clean the residues of the old gasket from the lower surface of the cylinder head.

1. The table gives the lower cylinder head surface planarity value.

Measurement

Value

1

Cylinder head lower plane planarity (mm)

< 0.1

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Refact the lower cylinder head surface without exceeding the minimum permitted height.

If necessary, both cylinder heads should be refaced.

The minimum permitted cylinder head height should never be exceeded, or else the actual cylinder head has to be replaced.

Measurement

Value

1

Minimum permitted cylinder head height (mm)

134.85 ÷ 135.15

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods
