Applies to: all engines; all production periods

While in motion with the roof tilted open, noise is heard from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise is heard only with the roof tilted open

Continue to Step 1

The noise occurs with the roof open

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

The noise occurs with the roof closed

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • Roof panel to runners;
  • Complete roof mechanism to the bodyshell

Continue to Step 2

Panel fastening faulty

Re-attach correctly Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r

Complete mechanism to bodyshell

Restore the correct fastening Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the sun-roof operating devices:

  • With the roof fully open, check that the felt pads beneath the front spoiler are present
  • With the roof fully open, check that the teflon mount between slides and rails is present
  • With the roof tilted open, check the clearance between central groove and slides
  • With the roof tilted open, check the pads sliding along the central groove are in good condition and properly fastened

Continue to Step 3

Sun roof fastenings and/or operating devices faulty

Restore the faulty components and the entire mechanism fixing Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists
