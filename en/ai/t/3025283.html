Removing

Refitting

Place the steering wheel in the centred position with the wheels straight.

Proceed with the removal 5580C18 air bag module - r+r .

Closely follow the safety regulations established for this operation.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the interconnection control unit.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and remove steering wheel (2b).

If necessary, use a suitable rubber hammer.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Cambio robotizzato

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit steering wheel (1a) in its seat so that references (1b) stamped on the steering wheel and steering column are aligned.

2. Tighten the fixing nuts to the recommended torque.
