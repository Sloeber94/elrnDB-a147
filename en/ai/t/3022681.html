Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition in the ON positino, when the front electric windows are operated, the acoustic signal is not heard

The acoustic signal confirming that the buttons have been pressed in the passenger compartment is not activated by the following controls: on the steering wheel, the radio console, ASR, CITY, hazard warning.
