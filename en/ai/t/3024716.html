Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Check that the tyre treads are not unevenly worn.

Some types of tyres are fitted with wear indicators; they have to be replaced as soon as these indicators become visible.

Do not cross-interchange the tyres.

Check that the tyre pressures are correct, including the spare tyre.

The tyre pressures should be checked on cold tyres.

Check that all the lights are working: headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, boot light, courtesy light, glove compartment light and instrument panel warning lights.

Check that the windscreen wash-wipe system is working correctly and if necessary adjust the jets.

Check that the windscreen and rear window wiper blades are correctly positioned and not worn.

Check operation of front disc brake pad wear indicator

Visually check:

  • bodywork exterior
  • underbody protection
  • Flexible and rigid sections of the pipes (exhaust - fuel system - brakes)
  • rubber components (dust excluders, hoses, bushes etc.)

Check and if necessary top up the coolant level 0010T20 coolant change .

Check and if necessary top up the brake fluid level 3330A10 BRAKE FLUID - Check level and top up, if necessary. .

Check and if necessary top up the windscreen washer fluid level.

Check and if necessary top up the battery fluid level.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Cambio robotizzato

Check and if necessary top up the Selespeed gearbox fluid level 2127C04 ELECTROHYDRAULIC selection gearbox hydraulic system FLUID - Check level and top up if necessary. .

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

Replace the engine oil and filter 0010T12 engine oil and filter replace .

Replace the brake fluid 0010T62 BRAKE and/or CLUTCH FLUID - Replace. fluid - Includes. washing pipes and bleeding air .

Replace the dust/pollen filter 5010B09 purifying filter (antipollen) filter element - replace .

When the service is complete, connect the Examiner tester to the tester input on the car and establish a connection with the VDO MANNESMAN INSTRUMENT PANEL.

Before carrying out INSPECTION SERVICE VALUE active diagnosis, check the instrument panel UNITS menu to ensure km or miles are selected according to the unit of measure used in your country

When under Active Diagnosis, run the INSPECTION SERVICE VALUE procedure to store/update the km interval at which the service is carried out.

When in the SERVICE menu on the instrument panel, for countries that use km measurement units, ensure that the kilometres displayed correspond to 20,000. A value of 12,427 should be displayed for countries that use miles as a measurement unit.
