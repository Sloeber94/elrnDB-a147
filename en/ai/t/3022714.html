Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During starting, the 'Air Bag failure' warning light does not come on (it should come on for 4 seconds to indicate that the initial control unit test is being carried out and then go out)

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) to the diagnostic socket and check:

  • that there are no faults in the AIR BAG control unit.

Continue to Step 2

Faults in Air Bag control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the equipment (Examiner or Examiner plus) to the diagnostic sockect and check:

  • that there are no faults in the instrument panel control unit.

Continue to Step 3

There are faults in the instrument panel

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

3

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • connection between Facia Control Unit (EOBD), Body Computer and Air Bag Control Unit
  • connection between air bag control unit and instrument panel
  • correct power supply to air bag control unit and instrument panel
  • See E7030 AIRBAG

Continue to Step 4

Wiring faulty

Change wiring

Incorrect power supply

Restore correct power supply See E7030 AIRBAG

4

AIR BAG CONTROL UNIT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check it is working properly.

Warning light bulb does not come on at all

Replace air bag control unit Op. 5580C14 air bag ecu - r+r
