Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the side lights switched on, when the lever to the right of the steering wheel is operated, the headlamp washer function does not work properly.

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the windscreen wipers are working properly

Continue to Step 1

The windscreen wipers are also not working

See table J01 WINDSCREEN/REARSCREEN WASHERS NOT WORKING

1

HEADLAMP WASHER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the following in order:

  • headlamp washer fuse and relay on engine bay control unit
  • headlamp washer pump power supply;
  • connection between stalk unit and Body Computer;
  • connection between headlamp washer relay Body Computer; See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 2

Component defective (relay fuse)

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not in place

Restore connections See E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER

2

CHECKING ELECTRIC HEADLAMP WASHER PUMP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the electric headlamp washer pump

Continue to Step 3

Electric pump faulty

Replace the electric headlamp washer pump Op. 5050D20 headlamp washer pump - r+r
