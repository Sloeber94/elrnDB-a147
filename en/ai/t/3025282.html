4110A11 steering wheel - r+r for replacement or check for versions with air bag

Removing

Refitting

Place the steering wheel in the centred position with the wheels straight.

Proceed with removal 5580C18 air bag module - r+r .

Closely observe the safety regulations established for this operation.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the horn.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the steering wheel (1b).

If necessary, use a suitable rubber hammer.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit steering wheel (1a) in its seat so that references (1b) stamped on the steering wheel and steering column are aligned.

2. Tighten the fixing nuts to the recommended torque.
