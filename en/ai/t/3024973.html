Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the oil vapour recovery pipe from the hose.

2. Loosen the bands (2a) and remove the hose between resonator and throttle body (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the hose between the resonator and the throttle casing back in its housing and secure it using the bands.

Connect the oil vapour recovery pipe to the hose and tighten the band.
