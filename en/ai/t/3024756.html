Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Tighten the bolts fixing the catalytic converter to the intermediate exhaust silencer.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the catalytic converter flexible mounting brackets.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the rear rigid power unit support.

2. Undo the bolt securing the power unit rear rigid mounting to the flexible mounting.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Lower the power unit rigid rear support, as necessary, using the hydraulic jack.

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the protective heat shield (1b) for the flexible mounting.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the power unit rear support flexible mounting (2b), complete with shim (2c) and nut (2d).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: 103 CV

Refitting

Removing

1. Place a new flexible mounting for the power unit rear support (1a), complete with shim (1b) and nut (1c), back in its housing and then secure it by tightening the bolts (1d) to the recommended torque.
