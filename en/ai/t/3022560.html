When the alarm is operated the siren does not emit auditory signals

All OK

Problems met

Operation

The auditory signals may or may not be activated, according to the various country modes. For more details Characteristic of working principle 5580E anti-theft device

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check whether there are faults on the CAN network

Continue to Step 2

Presence of faults in the CAN network

Restore the connections on the nodes concerned
