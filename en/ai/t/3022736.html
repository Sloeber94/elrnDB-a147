Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the area of the dashboard.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Road test the vehicle to try and identify the area where the creaking is coming from. For example, press the various parts described, checking whether the creaking disappears

Move directly onto the step involved

End of diagnosis

The creaking is not located

Continue to Step 1

Noise coming from one of the front doors

See table S62 FRONT DOORS CREAKING

1

GLOVE COMPARTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the glove compartment and the flap are correctly fixed in the dashboard: in particular, make sure that the rubber mounting is fitted

Continue to Step 2

Glove compartment fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing of the glove compartment in the dashboard.

Flap fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing of the compartment flap in the dashboard. Op. 7040A14 flap of glove compartment trim element (one) - arrange

2

DASHBOARD/WINDSCREEN MATCHING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the gap between the lower area of the windscreen and the dashboard is correct: it should be possible to pass a piece of card through this opening

Continue to Step 3

Dashboard fixing faulty

Restore correct fixing

3

CHECKING VENT FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the tabs fixing the air vents to the dashboard

Continue to Step 4

Faulty fastening of upper side vents.

Restore the correct fastening Op. 5010C20 Upper dashboard air VENT (one) left or right for demisting front door window - R.R.

Faulty side vents fastening

Restore the correct fastening Op. 5010C30

Upper outlet fastening fault

Restore the correct fastening Op. 5010C24 upper air outlet on facia - r r

4

CHECKING INSTRUMENT FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that instrument panel behind the steering wheel is correctly fixed: in particular, make sure that the felt is fitted in the dashboard matching area

Continue to Step 5

Instrument fastenings behind the steering wheel faulty

Restore the correct instrument fastening Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

5

CHECKING STEERING COLUMN COVER FASTENING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column cover is correctly fixed to the dashboard

in particular, make sure that the following are present:

  • insulating material around the cables;
  • felt in the matching area with the dashboard;
  • free movement of the steering wheel height adjustment device;
  • Steering column cover casing fixings

Continue to Step 6

Fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing to the dashboard. Refit the felt pads if necessary Op. 7040A26 steering column trim - r+r

6

CLIMATE CONTROL CONTROLS FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the heater controls are correctly fixed onto the dashboard

Continue to Step 7

Heater controls fixing faulty

Restore the correct fastening Op. 5040D15 control unit with automatic duel zone air conditioner knobs and buttons r r

7

SOUND INSULATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the facia Op. 7040A10 instrument facia trim - r+r

Continue to Step 8

Faulty fixing / soundproofing missing

Restore the correct fastening Op. 7040A10 instrument facia trim - r+r

8

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
