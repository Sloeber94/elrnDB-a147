Proceed with removal 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel .

1. Release the rigid pipe from the connector to the right rear brake from the attachments on the underbody.

2. Undo the connector (2a) and disconnect the rigid pipe from the connector to the right rear brake (2b), connector side.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the connector (1a) and disconnect the rigid pipe from the connector to the right rear brake (1b), right rear brake side.

Remove the rigid pipe from connector to rear right brake.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS, 2.0 TS; all production periods
