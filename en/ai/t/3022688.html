N22 HORNS NOT WORKING/WORKING OUT OF TUNE

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the centre of the steering wheel cover is pressed, the horns do not work or they sound out of tune

Results of the tests

1

HORNS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the horns.

Continue to Step 2

Horns faulty.

Repair the horns. Op. 5550B30 l/h or r/h horn (one) - r+r

Horns working out of tune

Replace the horns Op. 5550B30 l/h or r/h horn (one) - r+r

2

HORNS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Horns fuse
  • Horns relay
  • Horns earth
  • Continuity and insulation of electrical circuit between dashboard control unit and horns
  • See E3010 HORNS

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E3510 RADIO

3

CLOCK SPRING/STEERING WHEEL ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Connection between switches on steering wheel and clock spring.
  • Continuity and insulation of electrical circuit between clock spring and dashboard control unit
  • Operation of the switches
  • Operation of the clock spring
  • See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 4

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E3510 RADIO

Clock spring faulty

Replace the clock spring Op. 5580C12 clock spring for air bag module on steering wheel r r

Switches faulty

Replace faulty component

4

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
