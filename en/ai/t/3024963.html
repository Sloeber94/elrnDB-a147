1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the timing belt fixed tensioners (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the rear left timing belt guard (2b).

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the camshaft caps (3b) from the left cylinder head.

4. Remove the left cylinder head camshafts complete with oil seals.
