1

WIND NOISE AREA LOCATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle until the speed is reached when the problem occurs to locate the area from which the wind noise is coming (if necessary, use bodywork adhesive tape)

Continue to Step 2

The wind noise is coming from the aerial mounting

Check

  • gasket
  • seal
  • mounting distortion

The wind noise is coming from the rear view mirror

Verify:

  • gasket
  • mounting distortion Op. 7065C68 rear view mirror - r+r

The noise is coming from the front doors

Verify:

  • door alignment Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment
  • glass alignment [PR7005D15]
  • door surrounding seal [PR7005A34]
  • glass seal [PR7005D33]
  • outside glass scraper [PR7005D26]
  • handles [PR7005C11]

The noise is coming from the bonnet

Verify:

  • bonnet alignment Op. 7025A22 soundproofing lining for bonnet lid - r+r
  • wind noise buffers
  • front seal on crossmember [PR7025A62]
  • rear seal

The noise is coming from the windscreen

Verify:

  • perimeter seal
  • seal Op. 7035A14 (Bonded type) windscreen GLASS - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of glass and housing in bodyshell
  • fixed lid
  • windscreen wiper arm position [PR5050B38]

The wind noise is coming from the front bumper

Verify:

  • bumper alignment
  • front pillar seal
  • headlamp alignment
  • alignment/fixing
  • wheel arch seal Op. 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R.

The wind noise is coming from the spark out

Verify:

  • cable loom
  • seal
